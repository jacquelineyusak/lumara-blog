When financial administration is postponed, the work does not disappear. It becomes harder to remember, harder to verify, and more expensive in attention.

A monthly rhythm keeps the information close to the activity that created it. That makes questions easier to answer and patterns easier to notice.

## A useful close has three layers

First, complete the records: income, expenses, payments, and bank activity. Next, review the exceptions: missing receipts, unusual transactions, unpaid invoices, and items that need context. Finally, make a short note about what changed and what needs attention next month.

This turns bookkeeping into a feedback loop rather than a filing exercise.
