Lumara’s bookkeeping service starts from **IDR 2,000,000 per month** for a scope of **up to 100 transactions and two accounts per month**. Tax filings are quoted separately. These are Lumara’s published starting terms as of 9 November 2026, not a survey of market prices in Bali.

The final quote depends on the company’s workload and the support required. Use the scope alongside the price when deciding whether a service matches your business.

## What the starting bookkeeping scope covers

The published [bookkeeping service](https://lumara.id/services/bookkeeping) includes daily income and expense recording, document organization, transaction categorization, bank and cash reconciliation, accounts-payable tracking, general-ledger maintenance, monthly financial closing and tax-ready records.

Before accepting a quote, agree how the provider counts transactions and accounts, which period the work begins with, and what happens if the workload exceeds the quoted limits. Also confirm the files or reports you will receive and the information your team must supply.

These figures describe different services. Do not assume that a bookkeeping quote includes every tax return, registration or compliance task. Check the current [pricing page](https://lumara.id/services) and obtain a tailored written scope before engaging the service.

## What can change the quote?

A larger transaction volume, more accounts, incomplete records or historical work can change the amount of work involved. The same applies when the company needs several services coordinated together. Ask the provider to separate recurring work from any initial cleanup or other work outside the monthly scope.

For example, a company with two current accounts but several months of unresolved transactions should disclose that backlog when asking for a quote. The account count alone does not describe the work needed to establish reliable starting records.

## Five questions to ask before choosing a provider

1. Which transaction volume, accounts and periods are covered?
2. What records must the business provide, and by when?
3. What output will we receive after each monthly close?
4. Which tax filings, payroll tasks or compliance work are separate?
5. How will additional work be agreed and priced?

A useful comparison puts the answers side by side. A lower monthly amount is difficult to assess if one quote omits reconciliation or assumes that the records are already organized.

## Prepare for a useful pricing conversation

Share the company type, approximate monthly transactions, number of accounts, employee count and current condition of the records. Mention outstanding filings or earlier periods that need attention. Send sensitive supporting documents only through the agreed secure process.
