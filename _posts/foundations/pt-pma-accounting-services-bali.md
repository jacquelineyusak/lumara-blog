A foreign-owned company in Bali can have reporting work before it makes its first sale. A founder pays for the fit-out, the company signs a lease, or the first employee joins. Each event creates records the company may need for a different purpose. The fit-out can feed into the books and an investment report. The lease can trigger withholding tax. Hiring can bring payroll, employee tax and social security obligations.

In Indonesia, a PT PMA is an Indonesian limited liability company with foreign investment. Its bookkeeping, tax and corporate obligations belong to the Indonesian entity. A shareholder’s personal tax position depends on their own facts, including where they live, what they earn and how they receive it. For founders managing a company from abroad or living and working in Bali, it helps to establish both positions early. [Investment Law No. 25 of 2007, Article 5](https://peraturan.bpk.go.id/Download/29494/UU%20Nomor%2025%20Tahun%202007.pdf)

The rules and ordinary reporting dates below were checked on 21 November 2026. The calendar for your company depends on its financial year, tax registrations, transactions, employees and projects recorded in the government’s Online Single Submission system, known as OSS.

## What your PT PMA needs to manage

### Books that explain how money moved

Indonesian corporate taxpayers are generally required to keep books. The records must cover assets, liabilities, equity, income, expenses, sales and purchases. Keep the books, supporting documents and underlying electronic records in Indonesia for ten years. The default for statutory bookkeeping is Indonesian and rupiah. An English-language management report can help overseas directors review the business. For the statutory books, English with rupiah has a notification procedure, and qualifying companies that want English with US dollars generally need approval. [General Tax Provisions Law, Article 28](https://pajak.go.id/sites/default/files/2023-05/SDSN%202023%207.1_0.pdf) · [DJP rules on language and currency for bookkeeping](https://stats.pajak.go.id/id/peraturan/ketentuan-pemberian-layanan-administrasi-perpajakan-tertentu-dalam-rangka-pelaksanaan)

The early transactions are often the most revealing. If a founder pays a company supplier from a personal account, keep the invoice and proof of payment, identify who owes whom, and record any reimbursement. If money arrives from a shareholder overseas, keep the documents that establish whether it was an equity contribution, a loan or something else. An amount on a bank statement shows the payment. The supporting record explains what it was for.

Bali businesses often collect through several channels. A villa operator may receive a booking platform transfer after fees. A restaurant may see card settlements arrive days after the sale. A coworking business may collect a deposit before the service begins. Record gross sales, fees, deposits, refunds and settlement timing so you can reconcile the bank balance and explain what the business earned. At month-end, review outstanding supplier invoices and balances owed to or by shareholders. These checks make missing records easier to resolve while the transactions are still recent.

### Monthly tax in Coretax

Indonesia’s Directorate General of Taxes, or DJP, uses Coretax for tax administration. The monthly workload varies with the transactions. Employee pay can trigger Article 21 withholding, or Article 26 for relevant non-resident individuals. Specified payments to Indonesian suppliers can trigger Article 23. Certain rent payments on land and buildings can trigger final Article 4(2) tax. A payment to an overseas supplier or shareholder can raise Article 26 and treaty questions. The company may also have its own corporate income tax instalments under Article 25. [DJP’s consolidated Income Tax Law](https://pajak.go.id/sites/default/files/2023-05/SDSN%202023%207.1_0.pdf) · [DJP on tax on land and building rent](https://www.pajak.go.id/index.php/id/artikel/pph-atas-kontrakan-bukan-pajak-baru-apa-bedanya-dengan-rumah-kos)

| Monthly obligation, when applicable | Ordinary timing after the tax month ends | What to review |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Income tax withheld on payroll and covered payments | Pay by the 15th and file the applicable monthly return by the 20th of the next month | Payroll generally goes in the Article 21/26 return. Other covered withholding generally goes in the unified return, known as SPT Masa PPh Unifikasi. Keep the withholding slips with the working papers. |
| Article 25 corporate income tax instalment | Pay by the 15th of the next month | Validated payment generally fulfils the reporting requirement. Check the amount after the annual return and when first-year or other special rules apply. |
| Ordinary domestic VAT for a VAT-registered business, or PKP | File by the end of the next month, with payment of any amount due before filing | Reconcile sales, purchase tax invoices and credits. A registered business generally still files a monthly VAT return in a nil month. |

These are baseline dates. Certain payments, including some made in connection with imports or use of overseas services, can have different procedures or deadlines. Check official holiday rules and any period-specific announcements. A month with zero sales can still have payroll, rent, overseas invoices or a VAT filing. Where neither relevant withholding nor self-payment arose, a unified return is generally unnecessary, subject to the rules for the actual period. [DJP Coretax payment and filing deadlines](https://pajak.go.id/coretaxpedia/batas-waktu-pelaporan-pembayaran) · [Ministry of Finance Regulation 81/2024, Articles 94 and 171–173](https://peraturan.bpk.go.id/Details/306614/pmk-no-81-tahun-2024)

### VAT and local taxes in Bali

Check what the company sells before deciding whether to charge VAT, called *PPN*. In general, entrepreneurs that exceed the IDR 4.8 billion annual threshold under the VAT registration rules must apply to become a PKP by the end of the accounting year in which they exceed it. Smaller eligible businesses can opt in. The statutory VAT rate is 12%, with an effective 11% on many standard non-luxury taxable supplies under the reduced tax-base rule. Luxury goods, exemptions, special schemes and some cross-border transactions need separate treatment. [Ministry of Finance on PKP registration](https://peraturan.bpk.go.id/Details/286921/pmk-no-164-tahun-2023) · [Ministry of Finance Regulation 131/2024 on VAT](https://peraturan.bpk.go.id/Details/311485/pmk-no-131-tahun-2024)

Bali hospitality businesses should also check **PBJT**, a local tax on specified goods and services. Covered accommodation and food-and-beverage sales can fall under PBJT, administered by the relevant regency or city. A restaurant serving meals, a villa operator providing accommodation and a company earning villa management fees may sell different services, even when they work at the same property. Review each revenue stream, where the service is supplied and the applicable local rules before configuring invoices and point-of-sale reports. [Regional Financial Relations Law, Articles 50–55](https://peraturan.bpk.go.id/Download/189520/UU%20Nomor%201%20Tahun%202022.pdf) · [Ministry of Finance rules on goods and services outside VAT](https://peraturan.bpk.go.id/Details/215547/pmk-no-70pmk032022) · [Badung’s amended local tax regulation](https://jdih.badungkab.go.id/index.php/produk-hukum/peraturan-perundang-undangan/peraturan-daerah/peraturan-daerah-kabupaten-badung-nomor-8-tahun-2025-tentang-perubahan-atas-peraturan-daerah-nomor-7-tahun-2023-tentang-pajak-daerah-dan-retribusi-daerah)

### Corporate income tax and the annual return

The standard corporate income tax rate is **22% of taxable profit**. Taxable profit starts from the company’s accounts and can change after fiscal adjustments for items such as non-deductible expenses or differences in depreciation. Under Article 31E, eligible domestic corporate taxpayers with gross turnover up to IDR 50 billion get a 50% rate reduction on the share of taxable profit attributable to the first IDR 4.8 billion of turnover. That can mean an 11% rate on all qualifying taxable profit where turnover is at or below IDR 4.8 billion. Above that amount and within the eligibility ceiling, the reduced rate applies to the qualifying portion. [DJP explanation of the standard rate and Article 31E](https://pajak.go.id/id/artikel/badan-usaha-justru-tidak-terbebani-dengan-pp-202026-ini-penjelasannya)

The **0.5% final tax on qualifying small-business turnover** is a separate regime. Government Regulation 20/2026, effective 22 April 2026, removed ordinary limited companies, including newly registered PT PMAs, from new eligibility. An ordinary PT that previously qualified may still be able to use the regime for the remainder of its original period under the transition rules. If your company has been paying 0.5%, check the original eligibility and expiry against current rules before carrying the same rate forward. [Government Regulation 20/2026 and its transition rules](https://peraturan.bpk.go.id/Details/349415/pp-no-20-tahun-2026) · [DJP’s October 2026 FAQ, questions 23–25](https://www.pajak.go.id/sites/default/files/2026-10/FAQ%20PP%20NOMOR%2020%20TAHUN%202026%20Penyesuaian%20Pengaturan%20di%20Bidang%20Pajak%20Penghasilan.pdf)

The annual corporate income tax return, **SPT Tahunan PPh Badan**, is normally due within four months of the tax year-end. For a December 2026 year-end, the ordinary deadline is **30 April 2027**. Pay any remaining income tax due, called Article 29 or PPh 29, before submitting. If more time is needed, a timely extension notice can give up to two additional months. It generally requires a provisional tax calculation, provisional financial statements and evidence of payment of any estimated shortfall. Where an audit is in progress, check the additional documentation requirement. A company with no sales should check its taxpayer status and filing obligations before treating the year as closed. [General Tax Provisions Law, Articles 3 and 9](https://pajak.go.id/sites/default/files/2023-05/SDSN%202023%207.1_0.pdf) · [DJP corporate extension procedure](https://www.pajak.go.id/coretaxpedia/pemberitahuan-perpanjangan-spt-tahunan-pph-badan) · [DJP on formal non-active taxpayer status](https://pajak.go.id/coretaxpedia/pengajuan-status-nonaktif)

Tax years from 2025 use the Coretax annual filing workflow. The current return uses a main form and annexes selected according to the company’s industry and answers about its transactions. Teams familiar with the old Form 1771 will see differences. Before filing, tie the annual figures to the final ledger, reconcile tax credits and instalments, and review transactions involving shareholders or related companies. For amendments filed in Coretax from 1 October 2026, DJP now uses a replacement method for covered returns, including annual corporate returns. Retain each version, the reason for the change and its supporting calculation. [DJP corporate filing guide](https://www.pajak.go.id/coretaxpedia/lapor-spt-tahunan-badan) · [DJP on the differences from Form 1771](https://pajak.go.id/index.php/id/berita/ajak-amankan-pelaporan-spt-badan-kpp-sidoarjo-utara-siarkan-berbincang-tentang-pajak) · [DJP on the October 2026 amendment rules](https://pajak.go.id/index.php/id/siaran-pers/permudah-pembetulan-spt-djp-papabrama-sosialisasikan-skema-replace-di-coretax)

If you’re catching up on older filings, DJP opened temporary administrative penalty relief in October 2026. It includes certain late or voluntarily corrected returns with tax due for annual periods through 2025 and monthly periods through September 2026. The type and amount of relief depend on which conditions you meet and when you act. The underlying tax remains payable. [DJP’s penalty relief eligibility and deadlines](https://www.pajak.go.id/id/psa)

### Payroll, employee tax and BPJS

An employer needs accurate employee data and a record of what it pays. A monthly payroll close should account for salaries, relevant allowances and bonuses, new hires and departures, deductions and payment dates. For permanent employees, Indonesian payroll withholding normally uses the monthly effective rate, or *TER*, during ordinary payroll periods. In the final tax period, usually December or earlier when employment ends, the employer reconciles the year’s withholding against the Article 17 calculation and prepares the employee’s annual withholding statement. [Ministry of Finance Regulation 168/2023](https://peraturan.bpk.go.id/Download/346093/2023pmkeuangan168.pdf) · [DJP explanation of the payroll withholding method](https://pajak.go.id/index.php/id/siaran-pers/perhitungan-pph-21-lebih-mudah-berikut-ketentuannya)

**BPJS Kesehatan** covers national health insurance. **BPJS Ketenagakerjaan** administers employment social security, including programmes for work accidents, death, old-age savings, pensions and job loss. Employers must register themselves and eligible workers in applicable programmes. Programme coverage and contributions depend on factors such as company size, wages and worker eligibility. Foreign nationals working in Indonesia for at least six months are covered by the participation requirement, with programme-specific eligibility. Foreign employees on shorter assignments are subject to an insurance requirement under the rules on employing foreign workers. The six-month BPJS rule is separate from the tests for personal tax residency. [BPJS Law No. 24 of 2011, Articles 14–15](https://peraturan.bpk.go.id/Details/39268/uu-) · [BPJS Employment guidance for wage earners](https://www.bpjsketenagakerjaan.go.id/penerima-upah.html) · [Government Regulation 34/2021, Article 8](https://www.peraturan.go.id/files/pp34-2021bt.pdf)

For a team in Bali, check the applicable minimum wage by workplace location and sector. Badung and Gianyar have different schedules, and sector-specific rates apply to some businesses. Put *THR*, the statutory religious holiday allowance, on the payroll calendar as well. Under the 2026 guidance, qualifying private-sector employees with at least one month of continuous service are entitled to it, with payment due at least seven days before their applicable religious holiday. [Bali labour agency wage schedules](https://disnakeresdm.baliprov.go.id/ump-umk/) · [Ministry of Manpower’s 2026 THR circular](https://jdih.kemnaker.go.id/peraturan/detail/2983/surat-edaran-menteri-nomor-m-3-hk-04-00-iii-2026)

For 2026, Indonesia has a temporary government-borne Article 21 income tax incentive for qualifying workers at employers in specified business classifications. Some accommodation, food-service and travel businesses may qualify. Check the employer’s registered main activity against the official list, then check each worker’s eligibility and the reporting conditions. The 2026 rules cover the January to December 2026 tax periods. [Ministry of Finance Regulation 105/2025](https://peraturan.bpk.go.id/Details/341778/pmk-no-105-tahun-2025)

### LKPM and ongoing company compliance

**LKPM**, short for *Laporan Kegiatan Penanaman Modal*, is the investment activity report submitted through OSS. Under the current investment licensing rules, a PT PMA is classified as a large business and generally reports quarterly. Once the company has its business identification number, or NIB, check its reportable activities and locations in OSS. Prepare the applicable report for each. During fit-out or other pre-operational work, a company can have a preparation-stage report even with no revenue. The report changes to the operational or commercial stage after the relevant readiness declaration in OSS. [Investment Ministry/BKPM Regulation 5/2025, Articles 26 and 284–287](https://peraturan.go.id/filespengundangan/peraturan-bkpm-no-5-tahun-2025.pdf)

Quarterly reports are ordinarily due by **15 April, 15 July, 15 October and 15 January of the following year**. On that timetable, the fourth quarter of 2026 is due by 15 January 2027, subject to any official adjustment. BKPM confirmed that the third-quarter 2026 window ran from 1 to 15 October and that the KBLI 2025 adjustment did not affect submissions for that window. Prepare investment realisation, workforce and project information, plus any operational data and issues the form requests. Reconcile this quarter’s figures with supporting documents and prior reports. Keep the OSS receipt and follow up on requests to correct or clarify the report. [Investment Ministry/BKPM Regulation 5/2025, Articles 285–287](https://peraturan.go.id/filespengundangan/peraturan-bkpm-no-5-tahun-2025.pdf) · [BKPM’s third-quarter 2026 announcement](https://bkpm.go.id/id/info/pengumuman/penyampaian-lkpm-periode-triwulan-iii-2026)

Outside LKPM, review your **KBLI** business activity codes, the actual activities and locations, and the permits and commitments that apply to each. OSS uses activity and risk level to determine the licensing requirements. Indonesia moved to KBLI 2025 during 2026. The official transition guidance distinguishes code changes that can be handled automatically from changes to the substance of a business activity that need review. Compare what appears in OSS against what the company does after it adds a business line, opens a location or changes how it operates. [OSS licensing portal](https://oss.go.id/id) · [BKPM guidance on the transition to KBLI 2025](https://bkpm.go.id/id/info/siaran-pers/seb-kementerian-investasi-dan-hilirisasi-kepala-bkpm-tentang-implementasi-penyesuaian-kbli)

In Bali, the provincial government announced in July 2026 that OSS access for new foreign-investment licence applications had been restricted for 18 specified activity codes. It also confirmed that existing licence holders still had to meet LKPM obligations. If you plan to add an activity or site, check the specific code and the current OSS position before committing to the expansion. [Bali Provincial Government announcement](https://www.baliprov.go.id/web/gubernur-koster-batasi-akses-oss-untuk-pma-di-sejumlah-kbli-lindungi-umkm-lokal-dari-persaingan-tidak-sehat)

Put the **company-law annual report** on the calendar alongside the tax return. Under Ministry of Law Regulation 24/2026, in force from 3 November 2026, directors present the commissioners-reviewed annual report to the shareholders’ meeting within six months after year-end. The shareholders’ approval is recorded in a notarial deed and submitted through the notary in the legal entity administration system, known as SABH, within 30 days of signing. Companies with assets or turnover of at least IDR 50 billion, and companies meeting other statutory triggers, must also arrange an audit. Check the company’s beneficial ownership record and its annual update separately. [Ministry of Law Regulation 24/2026, Article 16](https://peraturan.go.id/files/permenkum-no-24-tahun-2026.pdf) · [Company Law No. 40 of 2007, Article 68](https://peraturan.bpk.go.id/Download/29563/UU%20Nomor%2040%20Tahun%202007.pdf) · [Ministry of Law rules on beneficial ownership updates](https://peraturan.bpk.go.id/Details/314767/permenkum-no-2-tahun-2025)

### Payments to foreign investors and group companies

Before paying a dividend to a foreign shareholder, interest on a shareholder loan, an overseas management fee or a royalty, identify the recipient, the relationship and the reason for the payment. Covered payments to a non-resident recipient outside an Indonesian permanent establishment generally start from **20% Article 26 withholding on the gross amount** under domestic law. A tax treaty can change the result where the payment and recipient qualify. Current treaty procedures require checking the applicable treaty conditions, including beneficial ownership where relevant, and obtaining the prescribed residence documentation, such as Form DGT, in time. Overseas services or intangible rights used in Indonesia can also raise VAT questions. [Income Tax Law, Article 26](https://pajak.go.id/sites/default/files/2023-05/SDSN%202023%207.1_0.pdf) · [Ministry of Finance Regulation 112/2025 on treaty procedures](https://peraturan.bpk.go.id/Details/342347/pmk-no-112-tahun-2025) · [Ministry of Finance Regulation 131/2024 on VAT](https://peraturan.bpk.go.id/Details/311485/pmk-no-131-tahun-2024)

Related-party transactions also need to follow the arm’s-length principle. Keep agreements, invoices, evidence of services, calculations and the basis for the amounts charged. The requirement to prepare a formal transfer-pricing file has separate triggers that look at factors such as turnover, the size and type of the transactions and whether an affiliate is in a lower-tax jurisdiction. An overseas parent pays a software bill and recharges it to the Bali company, for example. The company should be able to show what it received, why the cost belongs to it and how the allocation was calculated before deciding the tax treatment. [Ministry of Finance Regulation 172/2023, Articles 3 and 16–19](https://peraturan.bpk.go.id/Download/347583/2023pmkeuangan172.pdf)

### Personal tax for expat founders and employees

Indonesia’s personal tax residency tests look at whether a person lives in Indonesia, spends **more than 183 days in any 12-month period** in the country, or is present in an Indonesian tax year with an intention to reside there. Intention can be evidenced by documents such as a qualifying residence permit or local employment or housing arrangements. If both countries’ domestic rules treat a person as resident, check the applicable tax treaty. A shareholder who lives abroad and a director working from Bali can have different personal tax obligations even if they own shares in the same PT PMA. [DJP Regulation PER-23/PJ/2025, Articles 3–4 and 11](https://www.pajak.go.id/id/peraturan/penentuan-subjek-pajak-dalam-negeri-dan-subjek-pajak-luar-negeri-0) · [DJP treaty directory](https://www.pajak.go.id/taxtreaty-mli)

Indonesian residents generally account for worldwide income. A limited rule allows qualifying foreign specialists and researchers approved by DJP to be taxed on Indonesian-source income for their first four tax years of Indonesian residency, subject to conditions. Pay for work performed in Indonesia can still count as Indonesian-source even if it goes into an overseas account. A residence review should cover travel dates, when Indonesian living or work arrangements began, who paid the income, and any foreign tax paid. For calendar-year individual taxpayers required to file, the ordinary annual deadline is 31 March of the following year. [Ministry of Finance Regulation 81/2024, Articles 442–447](https://www.pajak.go.id/id/peraturan/ketentuan-perpajakan-dalam-rangka-pelaksanaan-sistem-inti-administrasi-perpajakan) · [Bali DJP guidance for foreign nationals](https://stats.pajak.go.id/id/berita/perkuat-edukasi-kepatuhan-pajak-wna-kpp-singaraja-gandeng-penjamin-dan-agen-visa-di-buleleng) · [DJP annual filing deadlines](https://www.pajak.go.id/index.php/id/batas-waktu-lapor)

## What to bring to a first conversation

You can start with a short description of your company, the Bali locations or projects it operates, and the work you want help with. If there’s a deadline or a notice already pending, lead with that. For a useful first review, have these details on hand.

- **Company and projects.** Your financial year-end, NIB, business activities and project locations, plus any relevant licences or recent changes.
- **Books.** The last month closed, approximate monthly transaction volume, the number of bank and payment accounts, and any backlogs or missing records.
- **Tax.** Current NPWP and PKP status, recent filing periods, any Coretax access or signatory issues, and recurring payments that may need withholding.
- **People.** Headcount by location, pay cycle, hiring plans and the current BPJS registration position.
- **Outstanding work.** The last LKPM receipt, upcoming renewals, previous-provider handover items and any tax or compliance correspondence you need to address.

If you’ve just incorporated, we can discuss setting up the monthly routine. If you already have a team or provider, we can agree on a specific handover or a single service.
