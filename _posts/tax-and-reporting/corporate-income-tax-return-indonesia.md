Your Indonesian company has closed its books and paid income tax instalments during the year. To prepare its annual return, the finance team must work out how the tax rules apply to the figures in the accounts, check which payments and withholdings count as credits, and establish whether the company owes a balance.

The *Surat Pemberitahuan Tahunan Pajak Penghasilan Badan* (SPT Tahunan PPh Badan) is Indonesia’s annual corporate income tax return. This guide follows the return from the year-end figures to the filing acknowledgement, with particular attention to foreign-investment companies (PT PMA). [DJP Regulation PER-11/PJ/2025, Articles 80 and 84–85](https://pajak.go.id/id/peraturan/ketentuan-pelaporan-pajak-penghasilan-pajak-pertambahan-nilai-pajak-penjualan-atas-0)

## Who files an Indonesian corporate income tax return?

An Indonesian company subject to the corporate filing obligation, including a PT PMA, files a return in the company’s name. Its annual tax calculation is separate from the personal income tax returns of its directors or shareholders. A company with an active tax registration generally still files when it has no operations or when its calculation shows no balance to pay. If operations have ceased, confirm the company’s status with Indonesia’s Directorate General of Taxes (DJP) and the filing obligations for the relevant period. [DJP Regulation PER-3/PJ/2026, Articles 3–4](https://pajak.go.id/id/peraturan/tata-cara-penyampaian-penerimaan-dan-pengolahan-surat-pemberitahuan-0) · [DJP guidance for inactive businesses with active tax registration](https://pajak.go.id/id/berita/bersama-ta-radio-pajak-jateng-ii-ingatkan-pelaporan-spt-badan)

## When is the corporate annual return due?

The standard filing deadline is **four months after the end of the tax year**, or after the end of the part-year when a return is required for a part of a tax year. For the usual January–December tax year, the ordinary deadline is **30 April of the following year**. For a company using a tax year that ends on 30 June, the ordinary deadline is 31 October. Use the tax year that applies to the Indonesian company when setting its filing calendar, including when its foreign parent works to a different reporting cycle. [DJP Regulation PER-3/PJ/2026, Article 4](https://pajak.go.id/id/peraturan/tata-cara-penyampaian-penerimaan-dan-pengolahan-surat-pemberitahuan-0) · [DJP filing deadlines](https://pajak.go.id/id/batas-waktu-lapor)

If the financial statements or their audit are unfinished, a company may be eligible for up to two additional months to file. It must submit a valid extension notice before the ordinary deadline with the reason, provisional accounts and a provisional tax calculation. Include evidence of payment for a provisional shortfall and an auditor’s statement where applicable. Check the status of the notice and the revised date before relying on an extension. [DJP Regulation PER-3/PJ/2026, Articles 5–6](https://pajak.go.id/id/peraturan/tata-cara-penyampaian-penerimaan-dan-pengolahan-surat-pemberitahuan-0) · [DJP guide to corporate extensions in Coretax](https://www.pajak.go.id/coretaxpedia/pemberitahuan-perpanjangan-spt-tahunan-pph-badan)

## Prepare the calculation, payment and return

### Reconcile the accounts to the tax calculation

Start with the company’s final financial statements and the trial balance behind them. Prepare a *rekonsiliasi fiskal*, or fiscal reconciliation, showing how the amounts reported in the accounts are treated for tax. For example, compare book and tax depreciation, identify costs that may need to be added back and check which income is subject to ordinary income tax, final tax or an exclusion. Use the reconciliation and any eligible tax losses carried forward to determine taxable income and calculate the tax due. Select the return schedule that applies to the company’s sector. [Indonesian Income Tax Law, Articles 4, 6, 9, 11 and 16](https://www.pajak.go.id/sites/default/files/2023-05/SDSN%202023%207.1_0.pdf) · [DJP Regulation PER-11/PJ/2025, Articles 84–85](https://pajak.go.id/id/peraturan/ketentuan-pelaporan-pajak-penghasilan-pajak-pertambahan-nilai-pajak-penjualan-atas-0)

Gather any records that affect the treatment of a material item. For a foreign-owned business, that can include intercompany loan agreements, invoices for parent-company services and current shareholder details. Check which disclosures or supporting schedules apply to the company’s transactions. If the ledger or balances still need reconciling, start with the [year-end accounting guide](/blog/tax-and-reporting/year-end-accounting-services-indonesia). [DJP Regulation PER-11/PJ/2025, Article 85](https://pajak.go.id/id/peraturan/ketentuan-pelaporan-pajak-penghasilan-pajak-pertambahan-nilai-pajak-penjualan-atas-0)

### Check credits and any PPh 29 balance

Compare the income tax due for the year with the credits the company can substantiate. Typical records include validated payments for [PPh 25 instalments](/blog/tax-and-reporting/pph-25-corporate-income-tax-instalments-indonesia) and eligible withholding certificates for tax deducted by customers from the company’s income. Match the taxpayer, tax year and amount to the supporting documents before using the credit. A supplier withholding slip that your company issued records tax withheld from that supplier. [DJP guide to income tax credits](https://www.pajak.go.id/id/kredit-pajak)

A shortfall after eligible credits is Article 29 income tax, or *Pajak Penghasilan Pasal 29* (PPh 29). Under the ordinary rule, pay the balance before filing and by the applicable deadline, then save the validated payment evidence. The calculation can also result in a nil balance or show an overpayment. [Indonesian Income Tax Law, Articles 28–29](https://www.pajak.go.id/sites/default/files/2023-05/SDSN%202023%207.1_0.pdf) · [DJP guide to income tax credits](https://www.pajak.go.id/id/kredit-pajak)

### Submit through Coretax and save the receipt

DJP directs annual returns for tax year 2025 onward to Coretax DJP. Confirm that the person handling the return has the correct company access and signing authority. Check that the financial statements and applicable schedules are complete, reconcile any prefilled withholding or payment data against the company’s records and verify the tax year before signing and filing. [DJP announcement on annual income tax returns](https://www.pajak.go.id/id/pengumuman/pelaporan-surat-pemberitahuan-tahunan-pajak-penghasilan) · [DJP Regulation PER-3/PJ/2026, Articles 7–8 and 11](https://pajak.go.id/id/peraturan/tata-cara-penyampaian-penerimaan-dan-pengolahan-surat-pemberitahuan-0)

Save the *Bukti Penerimaan Elektronik* (BPE), the electronic filing acknowledgement, with the submitted return, attachments and any payment confirmations. If the acknowledgement is missing, check the submission status and resolve any outstanding validation. [DJP Regulation PER-3/PJ/2026, Articles 13–14](https://pajak.go.id/id/peraturan/tata-cara-penyampaian-penerimaan-dan-pengolahan-surat-pemberitahuan-0)

### Illustrative example of a foreign-owned trading company

A PT PMA that imports and sells fit-out supplies closes its books on 31 December. The finance manager reconciles the depreciation on its warehouse equipment to the tax calculation and checks which PPh 23 certificates relate to amounts customers withheld from the company’s income. The manager matches each PPh 25 instalment to a validated payment for that tax year and records those credits in the return.

The calculation leaves a PPh 29 balance. The company pays it before the authorised person files the return, then keeps the BPE with the final return and the payment evidence. The directors can trace the amount paid back to the calculation, and the preparer has a clear record for next year. [DJP guide to income tax credits](https://www.pajak.go.id/id/kredit-pajak) · [DJP annual Coretax guidance](https://pajak.go.id/id/lapor-tahunan)

## Common corporate return mistakes

- **Using the parent company’s deadline.** Check the Indonesian company’s own tax year and filing date.
- **Carrying book profit straight into the calculation.** Document the tax treatment of significant differences.
- **Using the wrong credits.** Check that each certificate or validated payment belongs to the company and tax year, and that it is eligible.
- **Leaving company access until filing day.** Confirm in advance who can act and sign for the company in Coretax.
- **Closing the task after payment.** Keep the filed return and electronic acknowledgement alongside the separate payment evidence.

## Frequently asked questions

### Is 30 April the deadline for every Indonesian company?

30 April is the ordinary deadline for a company whose tax year ends on 31 December. The general rule is four months after tax year-end. If the company uses a different tax year or files for a part-year, determine the deadline from the applicable end date. [DJP Regulation PER-3/PJ/2026, Article 4](https://pajak.go.id/id/peraturan/tata-cara-penyampaian-penerimaan-dan-pengolahan-surat-pemberitahuan-0)

### Does a PT PMA with a loss or no revenue still have to file?

An active corporate taxpayer generally still files an annual return. The company should reconcile its accounts and report its tax position even when it has no tax balance to pay. If the company has ceased operations or DJP has changed its tax status, confirm the requirements for the period before closing the filing task. [DJP guidance on active tax registration](https://pajak.go.id/id/berita/bersama-ta-radio-pajak-jateng-ii-ingatkan-pelaporan-spt-badan)

### Which documents should we have ready?

Start with the financial statements, trial balance and fiscal reconciliation. Add the relevant schedules, withholding certificates, validated instalment receipts and evidence for significant adjustments. Check which disclosures are required if the company has related-party transactions, and obtain any missing records while there is time to review them. [DJP corporate return schedules and guidance](https://pajak.go.id/id/lapor-tahunan)

### How do PPh 25 and PPh 29 fit together?

PPh 25 instalments are payments made during the year which generally count as credits for that tax year. PPh 29 is the balance that remains when annual income tax exceeds eligible credits. When the ordinary payment rule applies, the company settles that balance before it files the annual return. [DJP guide to income tax credits](https://www.pajak.go.id/id/kredit-pajak)

### Can we file later if the audit is unfinished?

A company whose audit is unfinished may extend the filing period by up to two months if it submits a valid notice before the original deadline. The notice requires provisional information and supporting documents, including evidence of payment if the provisional calculation shows a shortfall. [DJP Regulation PER-3/PJ/2026, Articles 5–6](https://pajak.go.id/id/peraturan/tata-cara-penyampaian-penerimaan-dan-pengolahan-surat-pemberitahuan-0)
