Your Indonesian company has filed last year’s corporate income tax return, and the finance team wants to know what it should set aside each month this year. Start with *Pajak Penghasilan Pasal 25* (PPh 25), Indonesia’s Article 25 income tax instalment. The calculation usually draws on last year’s tax figures, while this year’s payments go towards this year’s tax. [Ministry of Finance Regulation PMK 81/2024, Article 226](https://www.pajak.go.id/id/peraturan/ketentuan-perpajakan-dalam-rangka-pelaksanaan-sistem-inti-administrasi-perpajakan), [DJP guidance on tax credits](https://www.pajak.go.id/id/kredit-pajak)

This guide is for owners, directors and finance teams at Indonesian companies, including foreign-investment companies (PT PMA). It covers how instalments are set, what to check after paying and how to account for them at year-end.

## What is PPh 25 in Indonesia?

PPh 25 lets a company pay towards its own income tax during the year. Where an instalment is due, the company pays it directly. When it prepares its annual corporate income tax return (*SPT Tahunan PPh Badan*), it reconciles the instalments paid for that tax year against the income tax due. [Indonesian Income Tax Law, Articles 25 and 28](https://www.pajak.go.id/sites/default/files/2023-05/SDSN%202023%207.1_0.pdf)

The company may also have separate responsibilities when it pays employees or suppliers. For example, PPh 23 can require a company to withhold income tax on certain payments to Indonesian suppliers. Keep any tax the company withholds from others separate from the PPh 25 it pays on its own account. [PPh 23 guide for Indonesian businesses](/blog/tax-and-reporting/pph-23-withholding-tax-indonesia)

## Who needs to check their instalment amount?

An Indonesian corporate taxpayer should confirm whether it has a PPh 25 amount to pay and the rule used to determine it. For an established business under the ordinary rules, the starting point is the income tax due on its previous annual return, adjusted for specified tax credits. A company’s current month of sales gives useful cash flow information, while the instalment calculation follows the applicable tax rules. [PMK 81/2024, Article 226](https://www.pajak.go.id/id/peraturan/ketentuan-perpajakan-dalam-rangka-pelaksanaan-sistem-inti-administrasi-perpajakan)

The amount may be nil. For example, the ordinary rule for a newly registered taxpayer provides for nil instalments in its registration tax year, subject to exceptions such as certain business restructures and taxpayer categories. Changes in business conditions, prior-year losses and other circumstances can also affect the calculation. Review the company’s status, its latest annual return and any relevant tax notices before scheduling payments. [PMK 81/2024, Articles 226 and 235–237](https://www.pajak.go.id/id/peraturan/ketentuan-perpajakan-dalam-rangka-pelaksanaan-sistem-inti-administrasi-perpajakan), [DJP Regulation PER-11/PJ/2025, Articles 113–120](https://pajak.go.id/id/peraturan/ketentuan-pelaporan-pajak-penghasilan-pajak-pertambahan-nilai-pajak-penjualan-atas-0)

## How to manage PPh 25 each month

1. **Confirm the amount for the period.** Keep the prior-year return, tax computation and any notice or adjustment that affects the amount. Review when an updated calculation takes effect.
2. **Pay an instalment when due.** The ordinary deadline is the 15th of the month following the tax period. Where the due date falls on a recognised holiday, the payment rule generally moves it to the next working day.
3. **Check payment validation.** A validated PPh 25 payment is treated as satisfying the ordinary monthly reporting obligation. A taxpayer whose instalment amount is nil is exempt from that PPh 25 monthly reporting obligation. Specific categories of taxpayer can have their own reporting duties.
4. **File the evidence with the monthly books.** Match the validated receipt to the company, period and amount in your instalment schedule. Where the receipt has a state revenue transaction number (*Nomor Transaksi Penerimaan Negara*, or NTPN), retain it with the schedule. [PMK 81/2024, Articles 94, 100, 171(10)–(11) and 233](https://www.pajak.go.id/id/peraturan/ketentuan-perpajakan-dalam-rangka-pelaksanaan-sistem-inti-administrasi-perpajakan), [DJP guidance on PPh 25 payment validation](https://www.pajak.go.id/id/penghitungan-angsuran-pph-pasal-25)

### Illustrative example of a PT PMA approaching year-end

An established foreign-owned design consultancy in Indonesia has a PPh 25 amount to pay under the ordinary rules. Its finance manager adds each validated monthly receipt to the instalment schedule and reconciles it to the ledger. At year-end, the company closes its accounts, determines the income tax due for the year and includes the instalments it paid for that year as tax credits in its annual return. The reconciliation shows whether a balance remains payable. The annual filing is a separate step. [Indonesian Income Tax Law, Articles 28 and 29](https://www.pajak.go.id/sites/default/files/2023-05/SDSN%202023%207.1_0.pdf)

## Common PPh 25 mistakes

- **Basing the instalment on this month’s sales.** Confirm the company’s calculation basis and the period it applies to.
- **Keeping only a bank transfer confirmation.** Check that the tax payment was validated and save the official evidence.
- **Changing the instalment after a slow month.** Review whether the change in your business meets the conditions for an adjustment.
- **Counting tax paid for the wrong year.** Reconcile each payment to the period it covers when preparing the annual instalment schedule.
- **Treating monthly payment as the annual return.** Assign a separate owner and deadline for the annual tax calculation and filing.

## Frequently asked questions

### How is PPh 25 usually determined for a company?

For a company under the ordinary rules, the calculation starts with income tax due on the prior year’s annual return and adjusts it for specified credits. Special rules can change the amount or the period when it takes effect. Keep the return and working papers together so the amount can be checked. [PMK 81/2024, Article 226](https://www.pajak.go.id/id/peraturan/ketentuan-perpajakan-dalam-rangka-pelaksanaan-sistem-inti-administrasi-perpajakan)

### Does a newly registered PT PMA have to pay PPh 25?

A newly registered company may have nil instalments in its registration tax year. Check how the company was formed and whether any special taxpayer category applies. A company formed through a qualifying restructure may have a different result. Also confirm the annual corporate return and any other filings that are due. [PMK 81/2024, Articles 235–237](https://www.pajak.go.id/id/peraturan/ketentuan-perpajakan-dalam-rangka-pelaksanaan-sistem-inti-administrasi-perpajakan)

### Do I need to file a monthly PPh 25 return after paying?

Under the ordinary rule, payment validation satisfies the monthly PPh 25 reporting obligation. A nil instalment is exempt from that requirement. Save validated payment evidence for the annual reconciliation and check whether your company falls into a category with separate reporting requirements. [PMK 81/2024, Articles 171(10)–(11) and 233](https://www.pajak.go.id/id/peraturan/ketentuan-perpajakan-dalam-rangka-pelaksanaan-sistem-inti-administrasi-perpajakan)

### Can I change the instalment if business slows down?

An eligible company may apply for a reduction when its circumstances change and its projected annual income tax meets the applicable conditions. Use current financial records and an updated annual tax forecast to assess eligibility. Keep paying the amount due while you confirm when any new instalment amount takes effect. [DJP Regulation PER-11/PJ/2025, Article 119](https://pajak.go.id/id/peraturan/ketentuan-pelaporan-pajak-penghasilan-pajak-pertambahan-nilai-pajak-penjualan-atas-0)

### How do the instalments affect the annual corporate tax return?

Include PPh 25 instalments paid for the relevant tax year among the company’s tax credits in its annual return. The annual calculation shows whether the company owes a balance after eligible credits. If a balance is payable, settle it before filing the annual return. [Indonesian Income Tax Law, Articles 28 and 29](https://www.pajak.go.id/sites/default/files/2023-05/SDSN%202023%207.1_0.pdf)

Use the return guide when you are ready to reconcile those credits against the year’s tax and complete the filing. [Corporate income tax return in Indonesia](/blog/tax-and-reporting/corporate-income-tax-return-indonesia)
