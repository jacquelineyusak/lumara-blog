Say your villa business hosted guests in December and the booking platform paid you in January. Which year does the income belong to? Why is the payout smaller than the booking total? What about the deposit another guest paid in December for a stay in February? Your bank statement shows when money moved. Closing the books means tracing those movements to the underlying transactions so you can account for what the company earned, what it spent, and what it still owes at year-end.

A year-end accounting service brings the ledger for a full financial year up to date, checks the closing balances and prepares the financial statements and schedules behind them. Some engagements also cover the tax calculation and annual filing. Others end with a handover to the company's tax adviser. The directors may also need the accounts for the annual shareholders' meeting, and an independent public accountant may need to audit them. A business can use the same ledger for all of this work, but each person needs different documents and has a different deadline.

This guide is for owners and finance teams at Indonesian businesses, including limited liability companies (PTs), foreign-investment companies (PT PMAs), and smaller businesses working out which rules apply to them. Most examples use a 31 December year-end. Start with the dates and reporting requirements that apply to your own entity.

## What a year-end accounting service covers

Monthly bookkeeping records each month's transactions and resolves differences as they arise. At year-end, the accountant reviews how those months fit together. The December bank balance must agree with the December ledger, but the accountant also needs to know whether a loan carried forward from last year is still outstanding, whether the business can collect its oldest invoices, and whether costs invoiced in January belong to December. These checks affect the balance sheet and profit for the whole year.

| Work | What you should receive |
| --- | --- |
| Bookkeeping and catch-up | A complete ledger for the agreed period, account reconciliations, and a list of missing records or questions |
| Financial close | A reviewed trial balance, the financial statements agreed for your entity, schedules for significant balances, and explanations for closing adjustments |
| Annual tax preparation | A reconciliation from book profit to taxable income, a schedule of tax credits and instalments, and the return and applicable attachments if agreed |
| Filing and follow-up | A submitted return, proof of payment where applicable, the filing receipt, and any follow-up required |
| Audit or corporate reporting support | Records your appointed public accountant can test, or financial information for the directors' annual report and the notary's filing |

The company should agree who owns each step. If a bookkeeper hands over a ledger and a tax adviser finds a missing invoice, decide who updates the books, who checks the effect on the tax return, and who approves the final figures. This matters particularly when the parent company's finance team keeps its own ledger or when a new provider is taking over partway through the year. ## How to close the books step by step

### Start from the last reliable balance

Collect last year's approved financial statements and final trial balance. A trial balance lists the ledger accounts and their balances on a given date. Compare this year's opening balances with last year's closing figures, including retained earnings, tax balances, loans and unpaid invoices. A trial balance can add up correctly even when an invoice appears twice or a loan is recorded as income. Each significant balance needs an explanation of what it represents and evidence that supports it.

If several months are behind, start with the earliest incomplete month. Get statements for every bank account, payment gateway and wallet the company used, even accounts that closed during the year. Export the full ledger and identify gaps in invoice sequences or missing statements. Record which months have been reconciled, which can be completed with existing documents, and which depend on another person finding a record. Closing December alone leaves earlier errors in the year-end numbers.

### Follow the money from the sale to the bank

Reconcile each account by matching the ledger to independent records. For cash, compare the amount in the books with a count. For a bank account, match transactions and explain differences such as deposits in transit and bank fees. For a booking platform or card processor, match sales and refunds to its settlement report, then match the payout to the bank. Where one payout covers multiple sales, keep the settlement breakdown so another person can trace it.

**Example. A Bali villa receives a net payout.** Assume the villa sells a stay for IDR 10 million, the guest stays in December, and the booking platform collects on the villa's behalf. The platform deducts a fee of IDR 1 million and pays the remaining IDR 9 million in January. On an accrual basis and assuming the fee is deducted from the December amount owed, the December accounts show IDR 10 million in revenue, IDR 1 million in fees and IDR 9 million due from the platform. The January payment increases cash and clears the amount due. This example assumes no taxes or refunds in the payout. If your statements include those items, identify them before posting the settlement.

Tracking only the bank deposits would give this owner a smaller sales figure, hide the fee, and put the sale in January. Following the sale, fee and payout separately also helps the owner check whether the platform paid in full. Apply the same approach to transfers between business accounts. Both sides should clear against each other, with any transfer still in transit explained.

### Decide which period each transaction belongs to

The year-end cutoff is the point at which you assign transactions to the period they belong to. Under accrual accounting, check when the company delivered goods or performed services, when it received goods or services from a supplier, and what its contracts say about payments and refunds. Review invoices and bank activity on both sides of the year-end date. Many of the documents that explain December arrive in January.

| What happened | What to check in the December accounts |
| --- | --- |
| A guest paid IDR 4 million in December for a February stay | Assuming the villa has yet to earn the payment, it records cash and a liability for the advance. It recognises revenue when it provides the stay under the relevant policy. |
| A supplier invoices IDR 3 million in January for maintenance completed in December | If the cost was incurred in December and the amount can be estimated, accrue the expense and a liability in December. Match the January invoice against that amount. |
| The business paid IDR 24 million in December for insurance covering January to December of the following year | Record the payment as a prepayment at year-end under accrual accounting. Recognise the expense over the coverage period. |
| A catering order was paid for in December and delivered in December | Check that the sale, any payment fee and the related cost appear in the year being closed. |

Revenue recognition in the accounts and the timing of VAT, withholding or local tax can follow different rules. When a deposit is material, give the accountant the contract, payment date, invoice and service date so each obligation can be assessed using the right facts. [IAI accounting standard on sales and advance payments, SAK EMKM Chapter 14](https://www.iaiglobal.or.id/v03/files/file_sak/emkm/files/basic-html/page43.html)

### Check receivables, payables and money from owners

A receivables schedule lists who owes the company, how much, and for how long. Match it to the ledger and look at what was paid after year-end. If an invoice is long overdue or disputed, ask what the company reasonably expects to recover and whether the applicable accounting standard requires an adjustment. Check the tax treatment separately. A reduction in the expected value of a receivable in the accounts does not by itself establish a tax deduction.

A payables schedule lists what the company owes suppliers. Compare it with supplier statements and payments made after year-end. Check that unpaid bills appear once and in the right period. Investigate balances that look unusual, such as a supplier with an old debit balance. It could be an advance, a credit note, or a payment applied to the wrong invoice.

If a director paid for equipment with a personal card, record the acquisition and establish what the company owes the director, if anything, using the invoice, proof of payment and the arrangement with the director. If a shareholder transfers money to the company, use the agreement and corporate records to determine whether it is capital, a loan or a reimbursement. For an intercompany balance, compare it with the other entity's records and investigate differences in currency, timing or charges. Save that reconciliation even when the parties agree on the amount after year-end.

### Verify inventory, fixed assets, payroll and foreign currency balances

- **Inventory.** Count items you hold at the close date, or reconcile a nearby count to movements up to that date. Compare quantities with the stock ledger and investigate spoiled goods, missing items, supplier deliveries and transfers between locations. Closing stock affects both assets and cost of sales. If a restaurant omits stock that was still on hand, it may understate inventory and overstate the cost of what it sold.
- **Fixed assets.** Keep an asset register with the purchase date and cost, when the asset was ready for use, depreciation, and any disposal. Review which purchases to capitalise, meaning to record as an asset and generally expense over time through depreciation. Keep a separate tax depreciation calculation where fiscal rules differ from the book policy. [DJP guidance on tax depreciation and amortisation](https://pajak.go.id/id/penyusutan-dan-amortisasi)
- **Payroll and contributions.** Match the payroll register to net pay transfers and the balances owed for employee withholding and applicable social security contributions. Record amounts relating to the year under the applicable accounting policy. A payroll cost can have several components and the bank transfer to employees shows only net pay.
- **Foreign currency.** List foreign-currency bank balances, receivables and loans in the original currency. Retain the rates and working papers used to translate them under the applicable accounting policy. Give the tax adviser the underlying records if different rates or rules apply to tax reporting.

### Review, approve and carry forward the close

Ask the preparer to show each significant year-end adjustment, the reason for it, the source document and the person approving it. Compare this year's gross margin, payroll, operating costs and balance sheet movements with last year or the budget. An unusually high margin might reflect better pricing, but first check that supplier invoices and inventory adjustments made it into the correct period. If a balance is unresolved, record its amount, what evidence is missing and who will follow up.

Save the final trial balance and reconciliations with the approved statements. Make sure the next year's opening balance sheet agrees with the close. If a later audit or tax review changes the underlying financial statements, update the relevant records with an explanation and keep a trail from the earlier version to the final figures. This gives next year's preparer a clean starting point.

## What the annual financial statements tell you

**The balance sheet**, or statement of financial position, is a snapshot of assets, liabilities and equity at year-end. It answers questions such as how much customers owe you, how much of your cash relates to customer deposits, and how much debt remains. In the villa example, the unsettled booking is an asset and the advance for a future stay is a liability.

**The income statement**, also called the profit and loss statement, shows the year's income and expenses. It helps you see whether the business earned enough from its operations to cover costs. The villa's December revenue and booking fee belong here under the assumptions in the example.

**The cash flow statement** shows how cash moved through operations, investments and financing. A profitable company can run short of cash when customers take longer to pay or when it buys equipment. A cash balance can also rise because of a loan or a contribution from shareholders. The cash flow statement explains where the money came from and how it was used.

**The statement of changes in equity** explains movements in the owners' interest during the year, including profit or loss, capital contributions and distributions. **The notes** explain the accounting policies and provide details behind the totals. For a regular PT's annual company report, the financial statements include these five components and a balance sheet comparison with the previous year. [Indonesian Company Law, Article 66](https://peraturan.bpk.go.id/Download/29563/UU%20Nomor%2040%20Tahun%202007.pdf)

### Choose the accounting framework before you finalise the statements

Indonesia's accounting standards, known as SAK, are issued by the Indonesian Institute of Accountants (IAI). The framework includes SAK Internasional, SAK Indonesia, SAK Entitas Privat (SAK EP), and SAK for qualifying micro, small and medium entities (SAK EMKM). SAK EP replaced SAK ETAP from 1 January 2025 and is designed for eligible private entities preparing general-purpose financial statements. The minimum SAK EMKM package includes a balance sheet, income statement and notes, with comparative information. Eligibility for a framework and the documents a particular filing calls for are separate questions. If last year's report said SAK ETAP, ask the preparer which framework applies now and what the change means for the comparative figures. [IAI framework for financial reporting](https://web.iaiglobal.or.id/SAK-IAI/Kerangka%20Standar%20Pelaporan%20Keuangan%C2%A0Indonesia) · [IAI guidance on SAK EP](https://web.iaiglobal.or.id/Berita-IAI/detail/standar_akuntansi_keuangan_indonesia_untuk_entitas_privat) · [IAI standard on SAK EMKM financial statements](https://www.iaiglobal.or.id/v03/files/file_sak/emkm/files/basic-html/page20.html)

Check the bookkeeping method used for tax as well. Certain taxpayers may notify DJP to use a permitted cash method for tax purposes under Ministry of Finance Regulation 81/2024. It still accounts for items such as non-cash sales and purchases, stock and depreciation. A business using a simplified tax method should confirm how it will prepare the financial statements its accounting standard requires. [Ministry of Finance Regulation 81/2024, Articles 455–459](https://jdih.kemenkeu.go.id/api/download/637047be-3dba-4347-aba1-98fa7fd5ab3f/2024pmkeuangan081.pdf#page=309)

### Retain the records behind the statements

Indonesian corporate taxpayers, including PTs and CVs, generally must keep books, including when a final income tax regime applies. The statutory default is to keep the books in Indonesian and rupiah. If an overseas parent needs another currency or reporting format, ask what permissions or notifications apply before using that format for statutory bookkeeping. Certain individual business operators can use simplified records under separate rules. Check the taxpayer's legal form and registration before deciding what the business must produce. [General Tax Provisions Law, Article 28](https://www.pajak.go.id/sites/default/files/2021-11/SDSN%20UU%20KUP%20stdtd%20UU%20HPP.pdf) · [DJP definition of a corporate taxpayer](https://www.pajak.go.id/id/apa-pengertian-badan-menurut-ketentuan-perpajakan) · [DJP guidance on financial statements for corporate taxpayers](https://pajak.go.id/id/artikel/pahami-hingga-z-kewajiban-penyusunan-laporan-keuangan-bagi-wajib-pajak-badan)

Article 28 also requires the books and supporting records, including relevant electronic accounting records, to be kept for 10 years in Indonesia at the entity's domicile. Export the ledger, trial balance and schedules in a usable format. Store the source records that explain them, including invoices, bank and platform statements, loan or shareholder agreements, tax records and approval evidence. Make sure someone at the company can retrieve the files if an accountant changes or a software subscription ends. [General Tax Provisions Law, Article 28](https://www.pajak.go.id/sites/default/files/2021-11/SDSN%20UU%20KUP%20stdtd%20UU%20HPP.pdf)

## Prepare the handover for the corporate return

Give the tax preparer the approved financial statements, final trial balance and supporting schedules for the Indonesian taxpayer. If your team reports to an overseas parent, show how the local company's balances tie back to the group package. The corporate return is called the *SPT Tahunan PPh Badan*. For filing deadlines, tax credits and the steps in Coretax DJP, see the [guide to filing an Indonesian corporate income tax return](/blog/tax-and-reporting/corporate-income-tax-return-indonesia).

### Show what the preparer needs to reconcile

A fiscal reconciliation (*rekonsiliasi fiskal*) explains why profit in the accounts can differ from taxable profit. Give the preparer a record of items to review, including costs that may be disallowed, differences between book and tax depreciation, and income that may be subject to final income tax. If the business has used a special tax regime, record its eligibility for the relevant year. Keep book and tax depreciation schedules side by side so the preparer can trace each calculation to the same equipment, dates and costs. [DJP guidance on financial statements and fiscal reconciliation](https://pajak.go.id/id/artikel/pahami-hingga-z-kewajiban-penyusunan-laporan-keuangan-bagi-wajib-pajak-badan) · [Government Regulation 20/2026 and transitional rules](https://jdih.kemenkeu.go.id/dok/pp-20-tahun-2026/summary)

**Example. From IDR 500 million of book profit to taxable profit.** Suppose a company has IDR 500 million in book profit before tax. Its adviser determines that IDR 50 million of expenses must be added back and that allowable tax depreciation is IDR 20 million higher than book depreciation. With no other adjustments or available prior-year tax losses, taxable profit is **IDR 530 million**. The book profit stays at IDR 500 million. The company's handover should show which ledger accounts and schedules support both adjustments.

### Match tax payment records to the books

Collect validated [PPh 25 instalment receipts](/blog/tax-and-reporting/pph-25-corporate-income-tax-instalments-indonesia) and withholding slips from customers. Match each record to the company, invoice or payment, and tax period. If a customer pays IDR 98 million on a qualifying IDR 100 million service invoice and issues valid evidence of IDR 2 million in non-final Article 23 withholding, the company still records IDR 100 million of revenue, assuming VAT is outside the example. Track the withholding as a potential tax credit for the preparer to check. Tax withheld by the company from employees or suppliers belongs in a separate schedule so it can be reconciled to the company's remittances. [Indonesian Income Tax Law, Article 28](https://www.pajak.go.id/sites/default/files/2023-05/SDSN%202023%207.1_0.pdf)

### Flag group transactions and payments abroad early

A PT PMA may owe its parent money for setup costs, pay a group company for marketing or software, or have a loan in another currency. Gather the agreements, invoices, description of the work performed, how charges were calculated, and both entities' balances. The tax adviser can then assess withholding, whether any treaty position needs supporting documents, and which transactions must be disclosed. The company should assess transfer pricing for transactions affected by a special relationship. Where master and local files are required, they must be available within four months of tax year-end and a summary accompanies the annual return. [Ministry of Finance Regulation 172/2023, Articles 16–19](https://www.pajak.go.id/sites/default/files/2025-05/REGULATION%20OF%20THE%20MoF%20No.172%20year%202023%20-%20English.pdf)

### Agree who follows through on the return

Before the handover, agree who will prepare the return, resolve questions, approve the figures, authorise any payment and sign in Coretax. If an audit or closing adjustment is still pending, tell the preparer early so they can check the applicable deadline and any available extension. After filing, keep the submitted return, payment evidence and electronic filing receipt (*Bukti Penerimaan Elektronik*, or BPE) with the approved accounts. If a later review changes a figure, record the reason and retain both versions so the company can trace any amendment. [DJP guidance on the electronic filing receipt](https://www.pajak.go.id/coretaxpedia/bukti-penerimaan-elektronik-bpe-spt-tahunan)

## Check payroll, VAT, withholding and investment reporting

Periodic obligations keep their own deadlines while you prepare the annual accounts. Many monthly withholding taxes are generally payable by the 15th of the following month and reportable by the 20th. For ordinary VAT filings, payment where due comes before submission, and the usual deadline is the end of the following month. Check the rule for each payment or return, including holidays and any sector-specific requirements. At the close, compare each relevant return and payment with the ledger for its period. If they disagree, identify the transaction and decide whether the books, the return or both need a correction. [Ministry of Finance Regulation 81/2024 as amended](https://jdih.kemenkeu.go.id/dok/pmk-81-tahun-2024) · [DJP payment and filing deadline table](https://pajak.go.id/coretaxpedia/batas-waktu-pelaporan-pembayaran)

### Payroll and the final PPh Article 21 calculation

For permanent staff, the final payroll tax period is generally December for employees who stay through the calendar year, or their last month of employment if they leave earlier. During most periods, employers use a monthly effective withholding rate. In the last period, calculate the tax on the applicable full or partial year and subtract the tax already withheld. For example, if the tax for the year is IDR 3 million and the employer already withheld IDR 2.4 million, it withholds the remaining IDR 600,000 in the final period. Ordinary excess withholding is returned to the employee under the applicable rules. The employer provides the annual withholding slip, BPA1, within one month after the final tax period ends. [Ministry of Finance Regulation 168/2023, Articles 15 and 21](https://www.pajak.go.id/index.php/id/peraturan/petunjuk-pelaksanaan-pemotongan-pajak-atas-penghasilan-sehubungan-dengan-pekerjaan-jasa-1) · [DJP Regulation PER-12/PJ/2026](https://www.pajak.go.id/index.php/id/peraturan/perubahan-atas-peraturan-direktur-jenderal-pajak-nomor-11pj2025-tentang-ketentuan)

Build the payroll reconciliation from the employee register. Compare gross wages, allowances, bonuses and holiday allowances with employee deductions and net payments. Reconcile employer contributions and amounts deducted from staff with applicable BPJS bills and payment evidence. Investigate leavers who still appear in contributions data or tax slips that do not match payroll. A clean handover shows gross expense, the amount paid to staff, amounts paid to the authorities and any liability still open. [BPJS Ketenagakerjaan guidance for wage recipients](https://www.bpjsketenagakerjaan.go.id/penerima-upah.html)

### VAT, vendor withholding and local tax

If the business is registered for VAT as a *Pengusaha Kena Pajak* (PKP), reconcile VAT on sales and eligible VAT on purchases against the electronic tax invoices, or e-Faktur, and compare them with the monthly VAT returns. A purchase invoice can appear in the ledger before the relevant VAT credit appears in the return. Track the amount, period and reason. Eligible input VAT can generally be credited for the same period or up to three subsequent tax periods if the requirements are met. Check cancelled or replaced invoices, refunds and credit notes against the correct period. [DJP guidance on input VAT credits in Coretax](https://pajak.go.id/id/siaran-pers/pengkreditan-pajak-masukan-pasca-implementasi-coretax)

On the supplier side, consider a qualifying service invoice where the company withholds income tax. Match the full invoice to the smaller bank payment and the amount the company owes to DJP. Check the electronic withholding slip, or e-Bupot, against the relevant periodic return and evidence of payment. This explains both why the vendor received less than the invoice amount and why withholding remains payable until it is remitted. [DJP guide to periodic withholding returns in Coretax](https://www.pajak.go.id/sites/default/files/2025-01/Buku%20Manual%20Coretax%202024%20-%20Seri%20SPT%20Masa%20Unifikasi.pdf)

A villa, hotel or restaurant may collect local tax on certain goods and services, known as PBJT. Qualifying accommodation and restaurant supplies that fall within the local tax rules are outside national VAT. If you offer multiple kinds of services, check how each revenue stream is classified in the relevant regency or city. Match the reportable sales, any local tax collected, the local return and the remittance evidence. If a platform statement combines the room charge, tax, fees and refunds, follow each component before reconciling the net payout to the bank. [Law 1/2022 on central and regional financial relations, Articles 50–53](https://jdih.kemenkeu.go.id/api/download/770ecf1d-664b-48a1-88f4-8849b8ca7258/1TAHUN2022UU.pdf) · [Ministry of Finance Regulation 70/2022 on relevant supplies](https://jdih.kemenkeu.go.id/dok/70-pmk-03-2022/summary) · [Badung local tax filing guidance](https://bapenda.badungkab.go.id/pelaporan-sptpd-1101)

### LKPM investment activity reporting

Businesses subject to LKPM report investment activity through the government's Online Single Submission system (OSS). Covered medium and large businesses generally report quarterly and covered small businesses every six months. The ordinary deadline for the fourth quarter and second semester is 15 January of the following year. Confirm the reporting period and each applicable project in OSS. This timetable can require work before the annual close is finished. [BKPM Regulation 5/2025, Articles 284–287](https://peraturan.bpk.go.id/Download/394633/005%20th%202025.pdf#page=186)

Use the ledger and supporting documents to identify the investment reported for each activity and location. If a fit-out invoice covers two sites, allocate it to the relevant projects on a documented basis. Distinguish amounts added this period from cumulative amounts already reported, and collect the workforce and operational information the form requires. Save the submissions and monitor OSS for approval or requests to correct them. [LKPM preparation guide](/blog/compliance/lkpm-notes)

## Annual company reporting and statutory audits

### What changes with your legal form

For a regular PT, including a PT PMA, the directors present the annual company report to the general meeting of shareholders, known as the *Rapat Umum Pemegang Saham* (RUPS), within six months of financial year-end, after review by the board of commissioners. The report includes the financial statements, the company's activities, the commissioners' oversight and other prescribed information. Shareholders' approval is recorded in a notarial deed. Under Ministry of Law Regulation 24/2026, the directors submit the approval and annual report through a notary in the corporate registry (SABH) within 30 calendar days after the deed is signed. Plan the shareholders' meeting and notary handover using the financial year-end and the date of the deed. [Indonesian Company Law, Articles 66–69 and 78](https://peraturan.bpk.go.id/Download/29563/UU%20Nomor%2040%20Tahun%202007.pdf) · [Ministry of Law Regulation 24/2026, Article 16](https://peraturan.go.id/files/permenkum-no-24-tahun-2026.pdf#page=11)

A single-person PT (*perseroan perorangan*) submits its balance sheet, income statement and notes through SABH within six months of its accounting period-end. A CV is a partnership treated as a corporate taxpayer, so its financial close and annual tax return still matter. The RUPS and notary procedure described above applies to regular PTs. An individual operating a business in their own name generally reports through their individual return. Where eligible, they may notify DJP that they will use its deemed net income method (NPPN) and keep the required records. The legal form, the financial reporting standard and the tax calculation method each need to be identified. [Ministry of Law Regulation 24/2026, Article 27](https://peraturan.go.id/files/permenkum-no-24-tahun-2026.pdf#page=14) · [DJP definition of a corporate taxpayer](https://www.pajak.go.id/id/apa-pengertian-badan-menurut-ketentuan-perpajakan) · [DJP guidance on the NPPN notification](https://pajak.go.id/id/pengumuman/penyampaian-pemberitahuan-nppn-melalui-coretax-djp-tahun-pajak-2026)

### When to bring in a public accountant

Under Article 68 of the Company Law, a PT must have its financial statements audited by a public accountant if assets or turnover are at least IDR 50 billion, or if the company meets another statutory trigger. Those triggers include being a public company or state-owned *Persero*, collecting or managing public funds, or issuing debt acknowledgements to the public. If an audit is required under Article 68, the financial statements need the audit before shareholders can ratify them. [Indonesian Company Law, Articles 68–69](https://peraturan.bpk.go.id/Download/29563/UU%20Nomor%2040%20Tahun%202007.pdf)

Check any separate reporting rules before relying on one threshold. The government regulations on annual financial information also call for audited statements from certain companies, including PTs with assets of at least IDR 25 billion. The former Ministry of Trade procedure under Regulation 25/2020 was repealed in 2025, so confirm the reporting route for your entity. Listed and public companies should also check the phased implementation of the central financial reporting platform under Government Regulation 43/2025. Lenders or investors can require audited accounts under their agreements. [Government Regulation 64/1999, Articles 4 and 4A](https://peraturan.bpk.go.id/Download/43811/PP%20No.%2064%20th%201999.pdf) · [Ministry of Trade Regulation 26/2025](https://peraturan.bpk.go.id/Details/331163/permendag-no-26-tahun-2025) · [Government Regulation 43/2025, Article 39](https://jdih.kemenkeu.go.id/dok/pp-43-tahun-2025)

An audit provides an independent opinion on the financial statements based on audit evidence. The public accountant may ask for bank or loan confirmations, inventory count records, invoices from before and after year-end, and evidence of subsequent payments. If inventory is significant, contact the auditor before you schedule the count so they can plan their work. Work back from the annual tax filing deadline as well as the shareholder meeting, since the audited statements go with the tax return. The company and its bookkeeper prepare the records. The appointed public accountant conducts the audit, and DJP runs any tax examination. [IAPI auditing standard SA 200](https://spap.iapi.or.id/1/files/SA%20200/SA%20200.pdf) · [Ministry of Finance register of public accountants](https://find-profkeu.kemenkeu.go.id/Ap/PeriksaAp) · [General Tax Provisions Law, Article 4](https://www.pajak.go.id/sites/default/files/2021-11/SDSN%20UU%20KUP%20stdtd%20UU%20HPP.pdf)

## A year-end calendar and document checklist

For a 31 December close, set an internal calendar that starts before the year ends. Work backwards from the tax deadline, the shareholders' timetable and any group or audit deadlines. The following phases are working targets. Your official dates depend on the obligation, the entity and its reporting periods. [Working tax calendar template](/blog/tax-and-reporting/tax-calendar)

| When | What to do |
| --- | --- |
| Before year-end | Confirm the reporting dates, last reconciled month, in-scope accounts, person responsible for each filing, audit needs and access to Coretax. Schedule counts and ask for anything likely to take time. |
| First weeks after year-end | Collect statements and invoices, check the cutoff, reconcile any outstanding periods, and keep the January payroll and reporting work on schedule. |
| Once the ledger is ready | Review closing entries and draft statements. Give the tax adviser or auditor the statements, trial balance and schedules. Agree when they will send back questions or adjustments. |
| Before the applicable deadlines | Finalise the accounts and complete any required audit in time for filing. Approve the annual tax return, authorise any payment and verify submission. Complete shareholder and registry reporting on their own timetable. |
| After filing | Save the receipts and approvals, resolve any remaining questions and carry forward the agreed opening balances and instalment schedule. |

Start the handover with the legal entity name, financial year-end, tax registration details, prior-year statements and return, and a list of deadlines or official notices. Then gather the documents that let a preparer reconstruct the year.

- **Ledger and banking.** Full ledger and trial balance, statements for each account, cash counts, settlement reports and their reconciliations.
- **Sales and purchases.** Invoices, refunds, credit notes, customer deposits, supplier advances and schedules of unpaid balances.
- **Stock and assets.** Count sheets, movements near year-end, asset invoices, disposal evidence and depreciation schedules.
- **People and tax.** Payroll register, contribution bills, tax invoices and withholding slips, periodic returns, payment evidence and prior filing receipts.
- **Capital and loans.** Shareholder and intercompany schedules, agreements, funding records and evidence for unusual transactions.

If there are missing records, list each item with its period and explain who can obtain it. Send sensitive records through the secure channel you agree with your preparer. Use Coretax's permissions for access and signing. At the end of the engagement, ask for the approved statements, final ledger, adjustment schedules, any open issues and the filing evidence for submissions included in scope.
