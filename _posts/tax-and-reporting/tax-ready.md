Tax-ready records give the person preparing a report a clear trail from a transaction to its supporting documents. A useful handover identifies the date, amount, counterparty, business purpose, payment status and any unresolved question. It also makes clear which period the records cover.

Being organized does not by itself determine the tax treatment of a transaction. The preparer still needs to confirm how the rules apply to the company and the facts.

## Assemble a monthly handover pack

Start with a complete list of accounts and sources used by the business. A bank statement alone may not explain cash payments, unpaid invoices or the context of a transfer.

- **Sales:** invoices, credit notes, sales summaries and records of payment.
- **Purchases:** supplier invoices, receipts and explanations for unclear items.
- **Bank and cash:** complete period statements, cash records and reconciliation notes.
- **People:** the relevant payroll or payment schedules, with access restricted to those who need them.
- **Prior reporting:** working balances, earlier submissions and unresolved questions that affect the current handover.
- **Changes:** new accounts, unusual transactions, corrections and changes in business activity.

Agree the exact list with the preparer. Different services and company circumstances require different inputs.

## Make each transaction traceable

A simple transaction register can use these fields:

| Field | Example of useful information |
| --- | --- |
| Date and reference | Transaction date plus an invoice or document identifier |
| Counterparty and description | Who was paid and what the payment relates to |
| Amount and currency | The amount shown on the supporting record |
| Payment status | Paid, part-paid, unpaid or awaiting clarification |
| Source document | A link to the invoice or receipt |
| Review note | The specific question and the person who can answer it |

For an unexplained transfer, retain the original entry and request the missing context. Do not label it as a business expense simply to clear it from a working list.

## Reconcile and record exceptions

Check that statement balances, cash records and the ledger can be reconciled. Flag duplicates, missing documents and items from other periods. If the business and preparer agree an adjustment, keep the explanation and approval alongside it.

The handover is easier to use when it includes a short cover note: the period, the accounts included, what has been reconciled, what is missing and who is following up. This reduces repeated questions and makes the remaining work visible.

## Agree secure access

Send employee information, identity documents and account access through the secure process agreed with the provider. Do not put passwords in a transaction spreadsheet or upload sensitive files to a public inquiry form. DJP’s [Coretax resource page](https://www.pajak.go.id/id/reformdjp/coretax) includes official guidance on account roles and access for businesses; use the appropriate current authorization process.
