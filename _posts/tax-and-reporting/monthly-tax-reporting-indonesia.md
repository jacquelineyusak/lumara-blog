Your Indonesian company has paid salaries, settled supplier invoices and sent a fee to an overseas consultant. The bank reconciliation tells you where the money went. To work out what the business needs to report, you also need to know what each payment was for, who received it and which tax rules apply.

Monthly tax reporting in Indonesia can include payroll and other income tax withholding, value-added tax (VAT) and instalments towards annual income tax. This guide helps owners and finance teams at local companies and foreign-investment companies (*Perseroan Terbatas Penanaman Modal Asing*, or PT PMA) identify which obligations to review and what to prepare for each one.

## Which monthly tax obligations should your company check?

Start with the transactions your company actually makes and its tax registration status. *Pajak Penghasilan* is income tax, abbreviated PPh. *Pajak Pertambahan Nilai*, or PPN, is VAT.

| What happened this month | What to check | Records to start with |
| --- | --- | --- |
| You paid wages, allowances or a bonus | [PPh 21 on employment income](/blog/tax-and-reporting/pph-21-employee-income-tax-indonesia). Review the worker's tax status and the type of compensation. | Payroll register, employee tax details and bonus approvals |
| You paid an Indonesian company for management or other specified services | [PPh 23 on certain domestic payments](/blog/tax-and-reporting/pph-23-withholding-tax-indonesia). Check the service and the supplier's status. | Contract, itemised invoice and supplier tax details |
| You owe a fee, royalty, interest or dividend to a non-resident | [PPh 26 on payments to non-residents](/blog/tax-and-reporting/pph-26-withholding-tax-foreign-payments-indonesia). Check income classification, tax residence and any applicable treaty. | Contract, invoice, payment terms and treaty documents where relevant |
| You pay or receive rent for land or a building, or have a construction contract | [PPh 4(2) final income tax](/blog/tax-and-reporting/pph-4-2-final-income-tax-indonesia). Check whether the transaction qualifies and which party must remit the tax. | Lease or construction contract, invoices and applicable withholding evidence |
| Your business is registered for VAT as a *Pengusaha Kena Pajak* (PKP) | [PPN and monthly VAT reporting](/blog/tax-and-reporting/ppn-vat-indonesia-business-guide). Reconcile VAT on sales with eligible VAT on purchases and review the return. | Sales and purchase ledgers, tax invoices and other supporting records |
| Your company has an income tax instalment due | [PPh 25 corporate income tax instalments](/blog/tax-and-reporting/pph-25-corporate-income-tax-instalments-indonesia). Check the instalment due and retain payment validation for the annual reconciliation. | Prior-year tax return, instalment schedule and validated payment evidence |

The detailed guides explain the conditions for each tax. Indonesia's Directorate General of Taxes (*Direktorat Jenderal Pajak*, or DJP) publishes consolidated income tax and VAT legislation. Specific regulations cover final tax on land and building leases and on construction services. [DJP tax legislation](https://pajak.go.id/id/uu-hpp) · [PP 34/2017 on land and building leases](https://jdih.kemenkeu.go.id/dok/pp-34-tahun-2017/overview) · [PP 9/2022 on construction services](https://jdih.kemenkeu.go.id/dok/pp-9-tahun-2022)

## Keep calculation, remittance and reporting separate in your calendar

**Calculation** starts with classifying the payroll item, invoice or other transaction and documenting the amount due. **Withholding or collecting** means deducting tax from a recipient's payment or collecting it from a customer where the rules require it. **Remittance** is the payment of tax to the state. **Reporting** means satisfying the filing requirement, often through a periodic return called an *SPT Masa*.

The timing and evidence differ across taxes. A PKP generally files a VAT return for each period even without taxable supplies in that period. A validated PPh 25 payment can fulfil the instalment's periodic reporting requirement, and the company still reconciles the instalments in its annual income tax return. Keep the document that shows what was paid or filed for each period. [DJP periodic VAT reporting guidance](https://pajak.go.id/panduan-layanan-pajak/konten/pelaporan/2025/orang-pribadi/spt/spt-masa-ppn) · [PMK 81/2024 and its amendment history](https://jdih.kemenkeu.go.id/dok/pmk-81-tahun-2024) · [DJP reporting amendment, PER-12/PJ/2026](https://pajak.go.id/id/peraturan/perubahan-atas-peraturan-direktur-jenderal-pajak-nomor-11pj2025-tentang-ketentuan)

## Illustrative example for a Bali villa management company

A PT PMA pays staff wages and a bonus, receives a local company's invoice for managing bookings and agrees to pay an overseas consultant for a one-off project. Its finance team collects the payroll register and employee details for PPh 21, checks the substance of the local management contract for PPh 23 and reviews the consultant's contract and residence documents for PPh 26. The team also checks the company's PKP status, the VAT treatment of its own services and whether it has a PPh 25 instalment due. Each check starts with a different set of records, even when the transactions appear in the same bank statement.

## What to hand over each month

1. **Close the transaction records.** Reconcile bank and cash activity to the ledger. Match supplier invoices to contracts and payments, and payroll totals to the payroll register.
2. **Set aside transactions that need a decision.** Flag new suppliers, overseas fees, unusual pay items, leases, construction invoices and contracts that bundle several services. Record who will confirm the tax treatment and by when.
3. **Assign the work.** Agree who prepares the calculations, who authorises payments, who files the returns and who follows up on missing records. Track deadlines by obligation and period. [Monthly tax calendar template](/blog/tax-and-reporting/tax-calendar)
4. **Save the evidence together.** Keep the relevant invoices, contracts, calculations, withholding certificates, payment confirmations and filing receipts with the period they support. [Tax-ready records checklist](/blog/tax-and-reporting/tax-ready)

Use the records you reconcile each month to plan the company return and final-period payroll. If you also need to file a personal return, check its timing separately. [Annual tax reporting in Indonesia](/blog/tax-and-reporting/annual-tax-reporting-indonesia)
