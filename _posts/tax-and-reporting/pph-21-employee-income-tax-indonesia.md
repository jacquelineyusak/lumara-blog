Your Bali business has hired its first employees. You know what each person should receive on payday, but what does the company need to calculate, pay and report? In Indonesia, *Pajak Penghasilan Pasal 21* (PPh 21) is the income tax employers withhold on salary and other qualifying payments to individuals. This guide walks Indonesian employers, including foreign-investment companies (PT PMAs), through the records and payroll checks behind that obligation. [Ministry of Finance regulation PMK 168/2023, Articles 2–6](https://jdih.kemenkeu.go.id/api/download/e60a82e0-b218-40f5-9d18-b924aa1e11ce/2023pmkeuangan168.pdf)

## When PPh 21 applies to an Indonesian employer

An employer generally calculates and withholds PPh 21 on qualifying pay to Indonesian tax residents. The rules also cover some payments to individuals outside regular employment, including fees to certain independent professionals. Where an individual is a non-resident for Indonesian tax purposes, PPh 26 may apply to qualifying income from work, services or activities instead. Check the recipient's tax residence and the nature of the engagement before choosing a category. [PMK 168/2023, Articles 2–6](https://jdih.kemenkeu.go.id/api/download/e60a82e0-b218-40f5-9d18-b924aa1e11ce/2023pmkeuangan168.pdf) · [DJP tax residence rules, PER-23/PJ/2025](https://pajak.go.id/id/peraturan/penentuan-subjek-pajak-dalam-negeri-dan-subjek-pajak-luar-negeri-0)

For permanent employees, review base salary, overtime, allowances, bonuses and *Tunjangan Hari Raya* (THR), the religious holiday allowance. Benefits in kind, such as employer-provided accommodation, can also require a tax review. The rules provide exemptions for some benefits, so record what the company provided and how it was used. [PMK 168/2023, Articles 5 and 7](https://jdih.kemenkeu.go.id/api/download/e60a82e0-b218-40f5-9d18-b924aa1e11ce/2023pmkeuangan168.pdf) · [Ministry of Finance regulation on benefits in kind, PMK 66/2023](https://jdih.kemenkeu.go.id/dok/pmk-66-tahun-2023)

## How to organise the payroll calculation

1. **Confirm who's being paid.** Record each person's employment category, start or end date, relevant tax identification details and tax residence. Update the marital and dependant information used to determine the permanent employee's non-taxable income category, known as *Penghasilan Tidak Kena Pajak* (PTKP). [PMK 168/2023, Articles 8–9 and 13](https://jdih.kemenkeu.go.id/api/download/e60a82e0-b218-40f5-9d18-b924aa1e11ce/2023pmkeuangan168.pdf) · [DJP rules on withholding records, PER-11/PJ/2025](https://pajak.go.id/index.php/id/peraturan/ketentuan-pelaporan-pajak-penghasilan-pajak-pertambahan-nilai-pajak-penjualan-atas-0)
2. **Collect everything that makes up the month's pay.** Compare the payroll register with approved salaries, attendance and overtime, allowances, bonus instructions and any separately recorded benefits. Ask whoever approves compensation changes to send the backup before payroll closes.
3. **Work out the withholding and reconcile payroll.** Use the rules for the employee category and tax period. Check the payroll register against the ledger and bank transfers so the team can trace gross pay, net pay and any amount withheld. Keep employee-level calculations with the tax records. [PMK 168/2023, Articles 15–20](https://jdih.kemenkeu.go.id/api/download/e60a82e0-b218-40f5-9d18-b924aa1e11ce/2023pmkeuangan168.pdf)

For ordinary permanent-employee payroll, the employer generally uses the monthly effective rate (*Tarif Efektif Rata-Rata*, or TER) on gross income for periods before the final tax period. In the final period, the employer recalculates withholding under the ordinary progressive rules for the year or relevant part of it and accounts for earlier withholding. December is the usual final period. If an employee leaves earlier, their departure month is the final period for that employment. [Government Regulation PP 58/2023](https://jdih.kemenkeu.go.id/dok/pp-58-tahun-2023/overview) · [PMK 168/2023, Articles 13, 15 and 21](https://jdih.kemenkeu.go.id/api/download/e60a82e0-b218-40f5-9d18-b924aa1e11ce/2023pmkeuangan168.pdf) · [Ministry of Finance regulation PMK 81/2024 as amended, Article 171](https://jdih.kemenkeu.go.id/dok/pmk-81-tahun-2024)

## Illustrative example of a villa operator's payroll

A Bali villa management PT PMA employs permanent Indonesian housekeeping staff. In a busy month it pays their regular wages, overtime and a performance bonus. The finance team collects the approved overtime and bonus records, checks each person's details, and adds the pay items to the payroll calculation for the relevant period. It then matches the gross payroll and withholding to the ledger and the net pay to the bank transfers.

When a staff member leaves mid-year, the finance team brings that person's earlier payroll records into the final-period calculation and prepares their withholding certificate. For a 2026 payroll, an eligible tourism employer may also need to check whether the temporary government-borne PPh 21 incentive applies to particular employees. Eligibility and reporting have their own rules. [PMK 168/2023, Articles 15 and 20–21](https://jdih.kemenkeu.go.id/api/download/e60a82e0-b218-40f5-9d18-b924aa1e11ce/2023pmkeuangan168.pdf) · [Ministry of Finance regulation on the 2026 incentive, PMK 105/2025](https://jdih.kemenkeu.go.id/dok/pmk-105-tahun-2025)

## Paying and reporting PPh 21 each month

| Task | What the employer checks |
| --- | --- |
| Calculate and withhold | Confirm covered pay and the tax calculation for each recipient and period. Record any withholding and the amount owed to the tax authority. |
| Remit | Pay the tax due by the 15th of the following month under the ordinary deadline and keep the payment evidence. |
| File | Submit the monthly PPh 21/26 return (*SPT Masa PPh 21/26*) through Coretax DJP as applicable, generally within 20 days after the tax period ends. Save the filing acknowledgement separately from the payment receipt. |
| Document | Keep the working papers, supporting payroll records and applicable withholding certificates. Give permanent employees their final-period withholding certificate, known as BPA1, within one month after that period ends. |

The general payment and filing deadlines move to the next working day when they fall on a holiday covered by the rules. [PMK 81/2024 as amended, Articles 94, 100, 171 and 173](https://jdih.kemenkeu.go.id/dok/pmk-81-tahun-2024) · [DJP reporting amendment PER-12/PJ/2026](https://pajak.go.id/id/peraturan/perubahan-atas-peraturan-direktur-jenderal-pajak-nomor-11pj2025-tentang-ketentuan) · [DJP's Coretax resource guide](https://www.pajak.go.id/id/reformdjp/coretax)

For December payroll or an earlier departure, use the final-period guide to reconcile what you withheld and prepare the certificate. [Year-end PPh 21 reconciliation and BPA1](/blog/tax-and-reporting/pph-21-year-end-withholding-indonesia)

## Common payroll mistakes

- Using the bank transfer list as the payroll register, which leaves gross compensation and deductions unexplained.
- Leaving overtime, bonuses or holiday allowances in a separate spreadsheet that never reaches payroll.
- Reusing an employee's details after a change in tax status or overlooking the final calculation when someone leaves.
- Taking payroll software output at face value without reconciling it to the ledger and payment evidence.
- Marking payroll tax complete when the remittance is made and overlooking the return or employee withholding documentation.

## Frequently asked questions

### Does a PT PMA need to withhold PPh 21?

Generally, yes, when the PT PMA pays qualifying income to an Indonesian tax-resident employee and the calculation gives an amount to withhold. Build the assessment into onboarding and payroll. For each pay run, check what was paid and whether the resulting filing and record-keeping requirements apply. [PMK 168/2023, Articles 2, 6 and 20](https://jdih.kemenkeu.go.id/api/download/e60a82e0-b218-40f5-9d18-b924aa1e11ce/2023pmkeuangan168.pdf)

### Does PPh 21 apply to foreign employees?

It can apply to a foreign employee who is an Indonesian tax resident and receives qualifying employment income. An individual who is non-resident may fall under PPh 26 rules for employment income, subject to any applicable treaty. Relevant residence facts can include where the employee lives, their time in Indonesia and evidence of an intention to reside. [DJP tax residence rules PER-23/PJ/2025, Articles 3–4](https://pajak.go.id/id/peraturan/penentuan-subjek-pajak-dalam-negeri-dan-subjek-pajak-luar-negeri-0) · [PMK 168/2023, Articles 6 and 14](https://jdih.kemenkeu.go.id/api/download/e60a82e0-b218-40f5-9d18-b924aa1e11ce/2023pmkeuangan168.pdf)

### Does the employer file when the withholding comes to zero?

Yes, a month with covered payments can still require the PPh 21/26 return when the amount withheld is zero. In an ordinary interim month with no covered payments, filing is generally unnecessary. The final period has its own filing requirement, so check December and any period in which a permanent employee leaves. [PMK 168/2023, Article 20](https://jdih.kemenkeu.go.id/api/download/e60a82e0-b218-40f5-9d18-b924aa1e11ce/2023pmkeuangan168.pdf) · [PMK 81/2024 as amended, Article 171(5)–(6)](https://jdih.kemenkeu.go.id/dok/pmk-81-tahun-2024)

### Why can withholding change in a bonus month?

A bonus paid to a permanent employee generally forms part of gross income for the relevant tax period. In a month using the effective-rate method, the higher gross pay may move the employee into a different withholding band. Keep the bonus approval with the payroll records so the finance team can explain the change. [PMK 168/2023, Articles 5 and 15](https://jdih.kemenkeu.go.id/api/download/e60a82e0-b218-40f5-9d18-b924aa1e11ce/2023pmkeuangan168.pdf) · [Government Regulation PP 58/2023](https://jdih.kemenkeu.go.id/dok/pp-58-tahun-2023/overview)
