A Bali company sells and services espresso machines for cafés. Its customers start asking for VAT invoices, and its supplier already charges VAT on spare parts. Before changing how it bills customers, the finance team needs to know which sales and services attract VAT, whether the company needs to register and which purchases may give it a VAT credit.

Indonesia’s value added tax is called *Pajak Pertambahan Nilai* (PPN). This guide is for owners and finance teams at businesses operating in Indonesia, including foreign-investment companies (PT PMA). It explains how VAT registration, invoices and bookkeeping come together in the monthly return. [DJP consolidated Indonesian tax legislation](https://www.pajak.go.id/id/sdsn-undang-undang-perpajakan-edisi-2023)

## What is PPN in Indonesia?

PPN applies to certain transactions involving taxable goods and services. A business registered for VAT generally charges and accounts for VAT on its taxable domestic sales. Under the ordinary credit mechanism it can offset eligible VAT on purchases against VAT due on sales for the relevant tax period. If output VAT exceeds the input VAT it can credit, the business generally pays the difference. Specific rules can apply to imports and other transactions. [Indonesian VAT Law, Articles 4 and 9](https://www.pajak.go.id/sites/default/files/2023-05/SDSN%202023%207.1_0.pdf)

| VAT term | What it means in the books | What to reconcile |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Output VAT (*Pajak Keluaran*) | VAT due on relevant sales or other taxable supplies | Sales ledger, commercial invoices and the corresponding tax invoices |
| Input VAT (*Pajak Masukan*) | VAT on purchases or other qualifying acquisitions that may give the business a credit | Purchase ledger, supporting documents and eligibility for the credit |

Keep VAT separate from sales revenue in the ledger so the sales total and the tax collected can each be checked. A supplier’s commercial invoice helps explain the purchase. To support an input credit, check for a valid *Faktur Pajak* (VAT tax invoice) or an officially recognised equivalent, and confirm that the purchase meets the credit rules. [DJP guidance on output VAT](https://pajak.go.id/id/pajak-keluaran), [DJP Regulation PER-11/PJ/2025, Article 122](https://pajak.go.id/id/peraturan/ketentuan-pelaporan-pajak-penghasilan-pajak-pertambahan-nilai-pajak-penjualan-atas-0)

## Who needs to check PKP status?

*Pengusaha Kena Pajak* (PKP) is the status of a business registered with Indonesia’s Directorate General of Taxes (DJP) for VAT. If your company makes taxable supplies, check its turnover against the applicable registration rules and confirm when any registration takes effect. Eligible small businesses can choose to register voluntarily. A PT PMA uses the same starting questions about what it supplies, its turnover and its registration status. [Ministry of Finance Regulation PMK 164/2023, Articles 17–21](https://jdih.kemenkeu.go.id/dok/pmk-164-tahun-2023), [Indonesian VAT Law, Article 3A](https://www.pajak.go.id/sites/default/files/2023-05/SDSN%202023%207.1_0.pdf)

Businesses with several income streams should classify each one. For example, food and drink served by a qualifying restaurant and certain hotel or villa accommodation services may fall under local tax rules. A company that also supplies equipment or provides separately contracted services should check those transactions on their own facts. [Ministry of Finance Regulation PMK 70/2022 on supplies outside PPN](https://jdih.kemenkeu.go.id/dok/70-pmk-03-2022)

## How monthly VAT works in practice

1. **Classify sales.** Review what the business supplied and how VAT applies to it. Use the contract, order or service description when the invoice combines several items.
2. **Issue the required tax invoice.** Check the invoice timing and customer details, and match each tax invoice to the sale it supports.
3. **Review purchase VAT.** Match supplier tax invoices or recognised equivalents to recorded purchases. Flag missing or inaccurate documents and mark credits that need an eligibility check.
4. **Reconcile and calculate.** Compare the VAT working papers with the sales and purchases in the ledger. Review adjustments such as returns or cancelled invoices before you work out the balance.
5. **Pay and report.** Arrange any payment due and file the applicable return. Keep the final calculation, related evidence and filing receipt together. [DJP Regulation PER-11/PJ/2025 on tax invoices and VAT reporting](https://pajak.go.id/id/peraturan/ketentuan-pelaporan-pajak-penghasilan-pajak-pertambahan-nilai-pajak-penjualan-atas-0), [DJP guidance for monthly returns in Coretax](https://pajak.go.id/reformdjp/coretax-spt/)

### Illustrative example of a business servicing café equipment

A VAT-registered PT PMA in Bali sells and services coffee equipment for cafés and buys replacement parts from a local supplier. Assume the domestic equipment sales and repair services attract PPN and the company uses the ordinary input VAT credit mechanism. The finance manager matches the month’s sales and service fees to VAT invoices and records the output VAT separately from revenue. When spare-parts invoices arrive, the manager matches them to orders and checks whether the VAT qualifies for a credit.

One supplier tax invoice lists the wrong buyer details. The manager asks the supplier to cancel it and issue a new tax invoice with the correct buyer details. The working papers show which sales generated output VAT, which purchases supported a credit and which invoice needs follow-up. [DJP Regulation PER-11/PJ/2025, Articles 49 and 122](https://pajak.go.id/id/peraturan/ketentuan-pelaporan-pajak-penghasilan-pajak-pertambahan-nilai-pajak-penjualan-atas-0)

## Paying PPN and filing the monthly return

A PKP generally files a monthly VAT return (*SPT Masa PPN*) by the end of the following month, including a month with no transactions. If the return shows an amount due under the ordinary VAT mechanism, pay it before filing and by the end of that following month. Record the payment reference and filing acknowledgement separately. [Indonesian VAT Law, Article 15A](https://www.pajak.go.id/sites/default/files/2023-05/SDSN%202023%207.1_0.pdf), [PMK 81/2024, Article 171(13) and (17)](https://www.pajak.go.id/id/peraturan/ketentuan-perpajakan-dalam-rangka-pelaksanaan-sistem-inti-administrasi-perpajakan)

## Common VAT mistakes

- **Using one tax treatment for every sale.** Check each income stream, especially when a business sells both goods and hospitality services.
- **Claiming input VAT from incomplete records.** Confirm who made the purchase, why it belongs to the business and whether the tax document supports a credit.
- **Leaving adjustments out of the reconciliation.** Review cancellations, returns and invoice replacements alongside the original entries.
- **Stopping after payment.** Save evidence of payment and confirm the monthly return was submitted.
- **Skipping a quiet month.** Check the VAT filing calendar even when sales pause or no VAT is due.

## Frequently asked questions

### Does a PT PMA need to register for VAT in Indonesia?

It can. Start with the goods and services the company supplies and whether its turnover brings it within the registration rules. Eligible businesses can also choose to register voluntarily. Once you establish that registration is required or suitable, check the effective date so your billing and monthly reporting start in the correct period. [PMK 164/2023, Articles 17–21](https://jdih.kemenkeu.go.id/dok/pmk-164-tahun-2023)

### What is the difference between a commercial invoice and a Faktur Pajak?

A commercial invoice sets out what the customer owes for the sale. A *Faktur Pajak* records the VAT charge and must meet Indonesian tax invoice requirements. Reconcile the two records where both are issued. The tax invoice or a recognised equivalent can help support a buyer’s input VAT credit if the credit conditions are met. [DJP Regulation PER-11/PJ/2025, Articles 31–33 and 122](https://pajak.go.id/id/peraturan/ketentuan-pelaporan-pajak-penghasilan-pajak-pertambahan-nilai-pajak-penjualan-atas-0)

### Can a business claim input VAT on all purchases?

Only eligible input VAT can be credited. Review the business purpose of each purchase, the supplier’s tax invoice or equivalent document and the rules for the types of sales the purchase supports. A company with a mix of supplies that carry different VAT treatments may need a separate check on shared costs. [VAT Law Article 9 as amended by Law 7/2021](https://pajak.go.id/id/peraturan/harmonisasi-peraturan-perpajakan), [DJP input VAT guidance](https://www.pajak.go.id/id/pajak-masukan)

### Does a PKP need to file a return in a month with no sales?

Yes. PKP monthly VAT reporting continues during months without transactions. A month without sales can still include purchases or invoice adjustments. Reconcile the records for that period before deciding what goes into the return, then keep the filing acknowledgement with your monthly records. [PMK 81/2024, Article 171(13) and (17)](https://www.pajak.go.id/id/peraturan/ketentuan-perpajakan-dalam-rangka-pelaksanaan-sistem-inti-administrasi-perpajakan)

### What should I send my accountant for the monthly VAT return?

Send the sales and purchase ledgers, commercial invoices, VAT tax invoices, and details of any sales returns, cancellations or corrected invoices. Include import or overseas purchase records if they are relevant. Your accountant can then trace the figures to supporting documents and flag missing items before the return is prepared.
