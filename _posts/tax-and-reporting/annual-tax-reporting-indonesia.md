If you direct an Indonesian company and draw a salary from it, year-end can put several tax tasks on your desk. The company needs its annual income tax figures. The payroll team needs to reconcile what it withheld and give eligible employees their certificates. You may also have a personal return to prepare.

Indonesia calls its annual tax return the *Surat Pemberitahuan Tahunan*, or *SPT Tahunan*. This guide explains annual tax reporting in Indonesia for business owners, directors and finance teams, including those at foreign-investment companies (PT PMA). Start by identifying who is responsible for each step and which tax period it covers.

## Which year-end tasks apply to you?

| Task | Who handles it | Ordinary timing |
| --- | --- | --- |
| [Corporate income tax return (*SPT Tahunan PPh Badan*)](/blog/tax-and-reporting/corporate-income-tax-return-indonesia) | The Indonesian corporate taxpayer prepares its annual company figures, tax adjustments and relevant credits. | Within four months after the end of its tax year or part-year. For a tax year ending 31 December, the ordinary deadline is 30 April of the following year. |
| [Individual income tax return (*SPT Tahunan PPh Orang Pribadi*)](/blog/tax-and-reporting/individual-income-tax-return-indonesia) | The individual taxpayer reviews income, taxes paid or withheld, and applicable information about assets and liabilities. | Within three months after the end of the person's tax year or part-year. For a tax year ending 31 December, the ordinary deadline is 31 March of the following year. |
| [Final-period PPh 21 reconciliation and BPA1 certificate](/blog/tax-and-reporting/pph-21-year-end-withholding-indonesia) | The private-sector employer reconciles PPh 21 withholding for eligible permanent employees and gives them their certificates. | Give BPA1 to the employee within one month after the final tax period ends. This is usually December for a continuing employee and the departure month for a permanent employee who leaves earlier. |

BPA1 gives the employee withholding figures to check when preparing their own return. The employer's tax payment and periodic return also have their own deadlines. [DJP annual return regulation PER-3/PJ/2026, Article 4](https://www.pajak.go.id/id/peraturan/tata-cara-penyampaian-penerimaan-dan-pengolahan-surat-pemberitahuan-0) · [DJP individual annual return guidance](https://www.pajak.go.id/id/artikel/spt-tahunan-nihil-orang-pribadi-mitos-vs-fakta) · [DJP withholding amendment PER-12/PJ/2026, Article 7 as amended](https://pajak.go.id/id/peraturan/perubahan-atas-peraturan-direktur-jenderal-pajak-nomor-11pj2025-tentang-ketentuan) · [PMK 81/2024 as amended, Articles 94 and 171](https://jdih.kemenkeu.go.id/dok/pmk-81-tahun-2024)

## What to prepare before filing season

1. **Confirm the taxpayer and period.** Identify the company that earned the revenue, the employer that ran payroll and the individuals who may need to file. Check each taxpayer's applicable period and deadline. An individual working across borders may need to establish Indonesian tax residence before deciding what income to report. [DJP tax residence regulation PER-23/PJ/2025](https://pajak.go.id/id/peraturan/penentuan-subjek-pajak-dalam-negeri-dan-subjek-pajak-luar-negeri-0)
2. **Reconcile the company figures.** Start with closed accounts and the supporting schedules. Match tax instalments and withholding evidence to the right taxpayer and period. Document what still needs review before the corporate tax calculation. [Year-end accounting guide](/blog/tax-and-reporting/year-end-accounting-services-indonesia) · [PPh 25 instalment guide](/blog/tax-and-reporting/pph-25-corporate-income-tax-instalments-indonesia)
3. **Prepare the payroll handover.** Reconcile employment income and withholding with payroll records. Confirm the employee's identity and final period, then prepare the applicable certificate and check the employer's periodic filing. [Monthly PPh 21 guide](/blog/tax-and-reporting/pph-21-employee-income-tax-indonesia)
4. **Check submission and save evidence.** Confirm who signs and files each annual return. When filing electronically, save the *Bukti Penerimaan Elektronik* (BPE), the electronic receipt, with the submitted return and relevant payment evidence. [DJP Coretax annual filing guides](https://www.pajak.go.id/lapor-tahunan/) · [PER-3/PJ/2026, Articles 13–14](https://www.pajak.go.id/id/peraturan/tata-cara-penyampaian-penerimaan-dan-pengolahan-surat-pemberitahuan-0)

## Illustrative example of a PT PMA director on payroll

A Bali services company closes its books on 31 December. Its finance manager reconciles the company's accounts, tax instalments and credits before preparing the corporate return. The payroll lead checks the final-period withholding and gives eligible permanent employees their BPA1 certificates. The director collects their own certificate, records of other income and the information needed for their personal return. The company's payments and the director's personal withholding go into different tax reconciliations.
