Your employer has issued your annual withholding slip. You also earned consulting fees and hold investments overseas. Before you open Coretax, work out which income belongs in your Indonesian return and gather the records that support it.

Indonesia’s individual annual income tax return is the *Surat Pemberitahuan Tahunan Pajak Penghasilan Orang Pribadi*, usually shortened to *SPT Tahunan PPh Orang Pribadi*. It reports your income and tax payments for the year, along with the assets and liabilities the return requires. If you own or direct a foreign-investment company (PT PMA), the company’s annual return covers its own income and tax position. Review your personal salary, fees, distributions and shareholding separately from the company’s accounts. [DJP reporting regulation PER-11/PJ/2025, Articles 80–81 and 83–84](https://www.pajak.go.id/sites/default/files/lampiran/PER-11_PJ_2025_0.pdf) · [DJP amendment PER-12/PJ/2026](https://pajak.go.id/id/peraturan/perubahan-atas-peraturan-direktur-jenderal-pajak-nomor-11pj2025-tentang-ketentuan) · [Corporate income tax returns in Indonesia](/blog/tax-and-reporting/corporate-income-tax-return-indonesia)

## Who needs to file an Indonesian individual tax return?

Start with your tax residence and filing status for the year. Indonesian citizens and foreign citizens can both be Indonesian tax residents. Indonesia’s domestic tests consider whether you live in Indonesia, are present there for more than 183 days in any 12-month period, or are present in a tax year with an intention to live there. The tests are alternatives, so your arrival date, home and plans can matter even when you have spent fewer days in Indonesia. [DJP residence regulation PER-23/PJ/2025, Articles 3–4](https://www.pajak.go.id/id/peraturan/penentuan-subjek-pajak-dalam-negeri-dan-subjek-pajak-luar-negeri-0)

Once your residence status is clear, check whether you have a filing obligation. A return with no balance payable can still be required. The rules provide a filing exemption for some taxpayers below or at the applicable tax-free allowance (*Penghasilan Tidak Kena Pajak*, or PTKP). For example, the employment exemption covers someone with income from one employer, no business or independent professional activity, and annual net income at or below PTKP. Check all relevant income sources before relying on a Coretax notice, which uses the data the tax office already holds. [DJP reporting regulation PER-3/PJ/2026, Article 20](https://www.pajak.go.id/id/peraturan/tata-cara-penyampaian-penerimaan-dan-pengolahan-surat-pemberitahuan-0) · [DJP guidance on filing exemption notices](https://pajak.go.id/coretaxpedia/notifikasi-tidak-wajib-menyampaikan-spt-tahunan)

### What if I have income overseas?

Indonesia generally taxes individual residents on income from Indonesia and abroad. Gather statements for overseas employment, investments, rent or other income, and identify when you earned it. An overseas payer can still be paying for work carried out in Indonesia. Tax treaties, a possible credit for eligible foreign tax and a limited regime for qualifying foreign nationals with specified expertise can affect the result. The special regime has eligibility conditions, a time limit and a DJP application process. Get advice if you moved during the year, are treated as resident in two countries or may qualify for different treatment. [Indonesian Income Tax Law, Articles 4 and 24](https://www.pajak.go.id/sites/default/files/2023-05/SDSN_2023_3.0.pdf.pdf) · [PMK 81/2024 as amended, Articles 442–447](https://jdih.kemenkeu.go.id/dok/pmk-81-tahun-2024) · [DJP treaty texts](https://www.pajak.go.id/taxtreaty-mli)

## When is the individual return due?

The ordinary deadline is three months after the end of the tax year. For the calendar year ending 31 December 2026, that is **31 March 2027** under the ordinary rule. The date can differ if you have a shorter tax period or a valid extension. If the completed return shows a balance payable, settle it before submitting the return. [DJP reporting regulation PER-3/PJ/2026, Articles 4–5](https://www.pajak.go.id/id/peraturan/tata-cara-penyampaian-penerimaan-dan-pengolahan-surat-pemberitahuan-0) · [Indonesian Income Tax Law, Article 29](https://www.pajak.go.id/sites/default/files/2023-05/SDSN_2023_3.0.pdf.pdf)

## What records should I gather?

| What to review | Records to collect |
| --- | --- |
| Salary, bonuses and other employment income | Annual withholding slips from each employer, such as BPA1 for permanent private-sector employees, and payslips to resolve differences. |
| Other income and applicable tax paid | Fees, business records, rental and investment statements, other withholding evidence, tax payment receipts and foreign tax documents where relevant. |
| Assets held at year end | Bank and investment statements and records for personal property, vehicles or shares, including your stake in a company where applicable. |
| Debts outstanding at year end | Loan and mortgage statements or other records that show the balance still owed at the end of the tax year. |

Use the previous return as a starting point for the asset and debt lists. Compare it with this year’s purchases, disposals and loan balances. Include the income disclosures the form requires, including applicable final-tax income and income excluded from tax. DJP’s filing guidance describes the different reporting categories. [DJP guidance on annual return income categories](https://www.pajak.go.id/coretaxpedia/penghasilan-dalam-pelaporan-spt-tahunan-pph) · [DJP guidance on annual withholding slips](https://pajak.go.id/id/artikel/bukti-potong-saya-fitur-rekonsiliasi-data-spt-tahunan) · [DJP annual return guidance on year-end assets and liabilities](https://www.pajak.go.id/id/artikel/spt-tahunan-nihil-orang-pribadi-mitos-vs-fakta)

## How to file in Coretax and confirm receipt

1. **Prepare your account.** Check you can access your personal Coretax DJP account and sign electronically.
2. **Review the draft return.** Create the individual annual return for the correct tax period. Coretax can bring in some stored data, including withholding slips, assets, debts and payments. Compare it with your records and complete any missing disclosures.
3. **Submit and check completion.** If the return shows a balance due, settle it and verify that the return reaches the filed status. Save the submitted return and the *Bukti Penerimaan Elektronik* (BPE), the electronic filing receipt. Keep any payment receipt with them so you can substantiate both the payment and the filing. [DJP individual Coretax filing guide](https://www.pajak.go.id/coretaxpedia/lapor-spt-tahunan-orang-pribadi) · [DJP guide to pre-filled data](https://pajak.go.id/coretaxpedia/tombol-posting-pada-spt-tahunan) · [DJP electronic filing receipt guidance](https://pajak.go.id/coretaxpedia/bukti-penerimaan-elektronik-bpe-spt-tahunan)

## Common mistakes when preparing the return

- Using citizenship or a single trip to decide tax residence without checking the other tests.
- Copying the pre-filled figures without comparing them with withholding slips or other income records.
- Carrying forward last year’s assets and liabilities without updating purchases, disposals and outstanding balances.
- Mixing personal income or shareholder transactions with company revenue and expenses.
- Saving a payment receipt and overlooking the separate filing acknowledgement.

## Frequently asked questions

### Do I need to file if my employer already withheld PPh 21?

Your filing obligation depends on your individual circumstances. If you are required to file, use the employer’s withholding slip to report the employment income and substantiate the withholding. A fully withheld salary may leave no balance to pay, while income from more than one job or additional sources can change the annual result. [DJP guidance on returns with no balance due](https://www.pajak.go.id/id/artikel/spt-tahunan-nihil-orang-pribadi-mitos-vs-fakta) · [DJP guidance on annual withholding slips](https://pajak.go.id/id/artikel/bukti-potong-saya-fitur-rekonsiliasi-data-spt-tahunan)

### What if I changed jobs during the year?

Gather the withholding slips for the relevant period from your employers and compare them with the entries in Coretax. If a slip is missing or contains an error in your identity, earnings or withholding, contact the employer to resolve it before you file. [DJP Coretax guidance on employees who change jobs](https://pajak.go.id/coretaxpedia/bupot-pegawai-yang-pindah-tempat-kerja) · [PPh 21 year-end withholding guide](/blog/tax-and-reporting/pph-21-year-end-withholding-indonesia)

### Can I extend the deadline if I am still waiting for my withholding slip?

An employee with no business or independent professional activity may qualify. Send the extension notice before the normal deadline. The rules require supporting documents, including a statement from the employer, a provisional tax calculation and proof of payment if there is a provisional shortfall. [DJP reporting regulation PER-3/PJ/2026, Article 5](https://www.pajak.go.id/id/peraturan/tata-cara-penyampaian-penerimaan-dan-pengolahan-surat-pemberitahuan-0)

### Does tax paid abroad automatically reduce my Indonesian tax?

Foreign tax may qualify for an Indonesian tax credit subject to the rules for the income and jurisdiction. Keep the income statement and proof of the foreign tax, and ask your adviser to check the amount that may be credited and any effect of the relevant treaty. [Indonesian Income Tax Law, Article 24](https://www.pajak.go.id/sites/default/files/2023-05/SDSN_2023_3.0.pdf.pdf) · [DJP guidance on tax credits](https://www.pajak.go.id/id/kredit-pajak)

### What happens if I moved to or from Indonesia partway through the year?

A change in tax residence can affect which period and income you report. Write down the dates you entered or left, when your home or work arrangements changed, and when you earned the income. Check whether you have to file a return for part of a tax year and confirm the deadline for that period. [DJP residence regulation PER-23/PJ/2025](https://www.pajak.go.id/id/peraturan/penentuan-subjek-pajak-dalam-negeri-dan-subjek-pajak-luar-negeri-0) · [PMK 81/2024 as amended, Article 170](https://jdih.kemenkeu.go.id/dok/pmk-81-tahun-2024)

### How can I tell that my return has been filed?

Check that the return appears under filed returns and save the electronic filing receipt, or BPE, delivered to your registered email address. If the receipt is missing, DJP’s guide shows how to resend it from the filed return. Keep it with the final return and any payment receipt. [DJP electronic filing receipt guidance](https://pajak.go.id/coretaxpedia/bukti-penerimaan-elektronik-bpe-spt-tahunan)
