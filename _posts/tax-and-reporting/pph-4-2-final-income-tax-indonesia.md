A foreign-owned company in Bali leases part of its building to another business and pays a contractor to remodel a different part. Its rent receipts and contractor payments may both raise final income tax questions. The company’s role changes between the two transactions, and the contract helps determine who handles the tax.

**PPh Pasal 4 ayat (2)** (*Pajak Penghasilan Pasal 4 ayat (2)*, or Article 4(2) income tax) is Indonesia’s final income tax on specified categories of income. This guide focuses on qualifying land or building rentals and construction services, including the records PT PMA companies and other Indonesian businesses can use to assess each payment. [Indonesian Income Tax Law, Article 4(2)](https://www.pajak.go.id/sites/default/files/2023-05/SDSN_2023_3.0.pdf.pdf)

## What does final income tax mean?

The final tax settles the Indonesian income tax on the income covered by that rule. A company records that income separately when calculating ordinary taxable profit and reports it as applicable in its annual return. Final tax paid or withheld on the income is excluded from credits against ordinary income tax. Review each revenue stream under its own rules, especially if the business earns both rent and operating income. [Indonesian Income Tax Law, Articles 4 and 28](https://www.pajak.go.id/sites/default/files/2023-05/SDSN_2023_3.0.pdf.pdf) · [DJP guidance on Article 4(2), slide 111](https://www.pajak.go.id/sites/default/files/2026-09/Materi%20Aspek%20Perpajakan%20Parpol%2021092026.pdf)

## When should a business check PPh 4(2)?

### Land and building rentals

Check the rule if your company rents out or pays to lease land or all or part of a building. Start by reading what the agreement grants the tenant and identifying who receives the rent. The gross rental amount can include related fees such as maintenance, security and service charges, even if a separate agreement covers them. The land and building rental rule excludes income from accommodation services. A property may earn income from a lease or from providing guest accommodation, so review what each customer actually pays for. [Government Regulation PP 34/2017, Articles 2–4](https://www.pajak.go.id/id/peraturan/pajak-penghasilan-atas-penghasilan-dari-persewaan-tanah-danatau-bangunan)

### Construction services

Check the rules if your company supplies or buys construction work, construction consultancy or an integrated construction service that combines them. The service category and the provider’s business or competency certification can affect the treatment. Review the work description, contract and available certification to determine how the service is classified. Construction work can include certain maintenance activities, so use the actual scope to assess a bill described as repairs or maintenance. [Government Regulation PP 51/2008 as amended, Articles 2–3](https://jdih.kemenkeu.go.id/download/0de381f7-2f69-4bd2-91d2-ad1f88560e4a/PP%2051%202008.pdf)

## Illustrative example of leasing part of a building in Bali

A Bali property company signs a lease for office space with an Indonesian corporate tenant. The lease sets rent, and a separate schedule covers security and common-area maintenance. The finance team checks who has signed the lease, what part of the building is covered, which charges relate to it and who receives each amount. If the company receives qualifying rental income and the corporate tenant is the withholding party, the tenant withholds. The property company reconciles the rent, withholding evidence and amount received against the agreement. [PP 34/2017, Articles 2–4](https://www.pajak.go.id/id/peraturan/pajak-penghasilan-atas-penghasilan-dari-persewaan-tanah-danatau-bangunan)

## Illustrative example of hiring a contractor to renovate a venue

A PT PMA hires an Indonesian contractor to renovate its restaurant kitchen. The same project also includes a recurring equipment service plan. Before approving the first progress payment, the finance manager asks for the work scope, milestone approvals and any relevant provider certification. The company uses those records to determine which services fall within the construction rules and whether it must withhold from the contractor’s payment. ## Who withholds, pays and reports?

| Transaction | When the payer is required to withhold | When the payer is outside the withholding rules |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Qualifying land or building rent | The tenant withholds. | The recipient pays the final tax directly. |
| Qualifying construction services | The customer withholds at payment. | The construction provider pays the final tax directly. |

Confirm the tax category and the amount covered by its calculation rules. In the relevant tax period, the responsible party withholds and remits the tax or pays it directly, then completes the applicable reporting. Where the unified monthly income tax return (*SPT Masa PPh Unifikasi*) applies, record the filing acknowledgement separately from the remittance receipt. If your business receives a payment subject to withholding, retain the withholding evidence and reconcile it to the contract, invoice and receipt. [PP 34/2017, Article 3](https://www.pajak.go.id/id/peraturan/pajak-penghasilan-atas-penghasilan-dari-persewaan-tanah-danatau-bangunan) · [PP 51/2008 as amended, Article 5](https://jdih.kemenkeu.go.id/download/0de381f7-2f69-4bd2-91d2-ad1f88560e4a/PP%2051%202008.pdf) · [DJP reporting regulation PER-11/PJ/2025, Articles 14–16 and 21–23](https://www.pajak.go.id/sites/default/files/lampiran/PER-11_PJ_2025_0.pdf) · [DJP amendment PER-12/PJ/2026](https://pajak.go.id/id/peraturan/perubahan-atas-peraturan-direktur-jenderal-pajak-nomor-11pj2025-tentang-ketentuan)

For the rent and construction payments covered here, payment or remittance is generally due by the 15th of the following month. The applicable monthly return is generally due within 20 days after the tax period ends. A deadline that falls on a holiday covered by the rules moves to the next working day. [PMK 81/2024 as amended, Articles 94, 100, 171 and 173](https://jdih.kemenkeu.go.id/dok/pmk-81-tahun-2024)

## Common mistakes with final income tax

- Treating guest accommodation and a property lease as the same kind of income.
- Leaving lease-related charges out of the review because they appear on another invoice.
- Applying one withholding treatment to every bill from a contractor.
- Keeping only the net receipt and losing the tenant’s or customer’s withholding evidence.
- Counting final tax paid as a credit against ordinary corporate income tax.

## Frequently asked questions

### Does PPh 4(2) apply to villa rental income in Bali?

It can apply where the arrangement qualifies as a lease of land or a building. An operator providing guest accommodation needs to assess that activity separately because the rental rule excludes accommodation services. Start with the contract, the services provided, the party receiving the income and what the customer pays for. [PP 34/2017, Article 2](https://www.pajak.go.id/id/peraturan/pajak-penghasilan-atas-penghasilan-dari-persewaan-tanah-danatau-bangunan)

### Who pays final tax on a commercial lease?

A tenant that is required to act as a withholding party handles the deduction and remittance. If the tenant is outside those rules, the party receiving or earning the qualifying rent pays the tax directly. Confirm the tenant’s status and document who will handle payment and reporting before the first settlement. [PP 34/2017, Article 3](https://www.pajak.go.id/id/peraturan/pajak-penghasilan-atas-penghasilan-dari-persewaan-tanah-danatau-bangunan)

### Do all renovation and maintenance payments attract construction final tax?

The treatment depends on the actual work. Review whether the contract covers construction work, construction consultancy or an integrated service under the construction rules. Some maintenance work can fall within the construction classification. Use the work orders and provider’s certification to review the correct treatment before approving payment. [PP 51/2008 as amended, Articles 2–3](https://jdih.kemenkeu.go.id/download/0de381f7-2f69-4bd2-91d2-ad1f88560e4a/PP%2051%202008.pdf)

### Does paying PPh 4(2) settle all of the company’s income tax?

The payment settles income tax on the income covered by the relevant final tax rule. The company still needs to assess its other income and meet its applicable annual reporting obligations. Keep separate records for final-tax income and ordinary taxable income so the accountant can reconcile both at year end. [Indonesian Income Tax Law, Articles 4 and 28](https://www.pajak.go.id/sites/default/files/2023-05/SDSN_2023_3.0.pdf.pdf) · [DJP reporting regulation, Article 80](https://www.pajak.go.id/sites/default/files/lampiran/PER-11_PJ_2025_0.pdf)
