Your December payroll is ready, and the team has already withheld income tax each month. Now it needs to check what the company withheld for each permanent employee against the amount due for the year, settle any difference and issue the employee's withholding certificate.

This guide is for private-sector employers in Indonesia, including foreign-investment companies (PT PMAs), closing *Pajak Penghasilan Pasal 21* (PPh 21) payroll withholding for permanent employees covered by the resident rules. The same final-period process can come up before December when an employee leaves. [Ministry of Finance regulation PMK 168/2023, Article 15 and Annex I](https://jdih.kemenkeu.go.id/api/download/e60a82e0-b218-40f5-9d18-b924aa1e11ce/2023pmkeuangan168.pdf)

## When to reconcile PPh 21 and issue BPA1

For employees who stay through the end of the year, December is the final tax period. For an employee who stops working earlier, use their departure month. Make the final-period calculation for each employee and prepare the corresponding withholding certificate, or *bukti potong*. [PMK 168/2023, Annex I](https://jdih.kemenkeu.go.id/api/download/e60a82e0-b218-40f5-9d18-b924aa1e11ce/2023pmkeuangan168.pdf) · [DJP reporting rule PER-11/PJ/2025 as amended by PER-12/PJ/2026, Articles 6–7](https://pajak.go.id/id/peraturan/perubahan-atas-peraturan-direktur-jenderal-pajak-nomor-11pj2025-tentang-ketentuan)

Older payroll records and guides may call the certificate **1721-A1**. Current Directorate General of Taxes (DJP) rules call the private-sector permanent employee certificate **BPA1**, which you may see as **BP A1** in Coretax DJP. It records employment income and the tax position the employee can use when preparing their own annual income tax return. [Former 1721-A1 procedure, PER-2/PJ/2024](https://www.pajak.go.id/id/peraturan/bentuk-dan-tata-cara-pembuatan-bukti-pemotongan-pajak-penghasilan-pasal-21-danatau-pajak) · [Current DJP rule, PER-12/PJ/2026](https://pajak.go.id/id/peraturan/perubahan-atas-peraturan-direktur-jenderal-pajak-nomor-11pj2025-tentang-ketentuan) · [DJP guidance on checking withholding certificates](https://pajak.go.id/id/artikel/bukti-potong-saya-fitur-rekonsiliasi-data-spt-tahunan)

## How the final-period calculation works

In the periods leading up to the final period, employers generally apply a monthly effective withholding rate (*Tarif Efektif Rata-Rata*, or TER) to a permanent employee's gross pay. In the final period, calculate the PPh 21 due on taxable employment income for the year or relevant part of it using the ordinary progressive rates. Then deduct the amounts already withheld in the earlier periods to find the final balance. An employee whose Indonesian tax liability started or ended during the year can require a different calculation. [Government regulation PP 58/2023](https://jdih.kemenkeu.go.id/dok/pp-58-tahun-2023/overview) · [PMK 168/2023, Articles 8–11, 13 and 15](https://jdih.kemenkeu.go.id/api/download/e60a82e0-b218-40f5-9d18-b924aa1e11ce/2023pmkeuangan168.pdf)

If the employee's earlier deductions fall short, account for the balance in their final-period withholding. If the company withheld too much from the employee, it must generally return the excess with the certificate by the end of the month following the final period. Government-borne PPh 21 is excluded from that employee refund rule. Track the refund to the employee separately from any excess deposit the company has to reconcile with DJP. [PMK 168/2023, Articles 15 and 21](https://jdih.kemenkeu.go.id/api/download/e60a82e0-b218-40f5-9d18-b924aa1e11ce/2023pmkeuangan168.pdf) · [DJP reporting amendment PER-12/PJ/2026](https://pajak.go.id/id/peraturan/perubahan-atas-peraturan-direktur-jenderal-pajak-nomor-11pj2025-tentang-ketentuan)

## What to check before you close payroll

1. **Confirm the employee and final period.** Match the employee's name and tax identification details to the payroll and Coretax records. Check their start or leaving date, residency and the information supporting their non-taxable income allowance (*Penghasilan Tidak Kena Pajak*, or PTKP). [PMK 168/2023, Articles 8–9 and 15](https://jdih.kemenkeu.go.id/api/download/e60a82e0-b218-40f5-9d18-b924aa1e11ce/2023pmkeuangan168.pdf) · [DJP guidance on employer and employee withholding records](https://pajak.go.id/id/artikel/bukti-potong-saya-fitur-rekonsiliasi-data-spt-tahunan)
2. **Reconcile the year's pay and tax.** Bring together salary, overtime, bonuses, the religious holiday allowance (*Tunjangan Hari Raya*, or THR) and any taxable benefits. Compare the employee-level payroll register to the ledger, earlier period filings and payment evidence. Follow up on missing or inconsistent records before using any amounts that Coretax fills in. [PMK 168/2023, Articles 5, 7 and 20](https://jdih.kemenkeu.go.id/api/download/e60a82e0-b218-40f5-9d18-b924aa1e11ce/2023pmkeuangan168.pdf) · [DJP's Coretax guide to preparing A1](https://pajak.go.id/sites/default/files/2025-12/Pembuatan%20Bukti%20Pemotongan%20PPh%20Pasal%2021-Tahunan%20A1%20%20%281%29.pdf)
3. **Prepare, check and issue the certificate.** Use the eBupot module in Coretax DJP to prepare the final-period BPA1. Check the employee's identity, employment period, income and withholding against the approved calculation before signing. If an earlier payroll filing is wrong, check which certificate or return needs a correction. [DJP rules PER-11/PJ/2025, Articles 6–12](https://www.pajak.go.id/id/peraturan/ketentuan-pelaporan-pajak-penghasilan-pajak-pertambahan-nilai-pajak-penjualan-atas-0) · [DJP amendment PER-12/PJ/2026](https://pajak.go.id/id/peraturan/perubahan-atas-peraturan-direktur-jenderal-pajak-nomor-11pj2025-tentang-ketentuan)

## Illustrative example of a Bali employer closing payroll

A hotel management PT PMA has a front desk employee who works through December and a reservations employee who left in September. In September, finance checks the departing employee's pay and earlier withholding, completes the final-period calculation and adds the BPA1 handover to the offboarding list. In December, it performs the same checks for the front desk employee, including a performance bonus paid near year-end. For both employees, finance retains the calculation and certificate with the relevant month's payroll records.

## Keep the payment, filing and certificate dates separate

| Task | Who handles it | Ordinary timing or use |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Remit any PPh 21 payable for the final period | Employer | By the 15th of the following month. |
| File the final-period PPh 21/26 return (*SPT Masa PPh 21/26*) | Employer | Within 20 days after the period ends. The final-period filing requirement still applies if the amount payable is zero. |
| Give the employee the BPA1 certificate | Employer | Within one month after the final period ends. Arrange any required refund of excess employee withholding alongside the certificate. |
| File the annual individual income tax return (*SPT Tahunan PPh Orang Pribadi*) | Employee, when required | Use the BPA1 figures alongside information about other income, tax credits and other details relevant to the return. |

The ordinary remittance and monthly filing deadlines can move to the next working day if they fall on a holiday covered by the rules. Keep the payment evidence, filing acknowledgement and issued certificate so each step can be checked separately. [PMK 81/2024 as amended, Articles 94, 100, 171 and 173](https://jdih.kemenkeu.go.id/dok/pmk-81-tahun-2024) · [PMK 168/2023, Article 21](https://jdih.kemenkeu.go.id/api/download/e60a82e0-b218-40f5-9d18-b924aa1e11ce/2023pmkeuangan168.pdf) · [DJP amendment PER-12/PJ/2026, Articles 7 and 11 of the amended rules](https://pajak.go.id/id/peraturan/perubahan-atas-peraturan-direktur-jenderal-pajak-nomor-11pj2025-tentang-ketentuan) · [DJP guidance for individual annual returns](https://pajak.go.id/coretaxpedia/lapor-spt-tahunan-orang-pribadi)

## Common mistakes to avoid

- Leaving certificates for employees who departed earlier in the year until December.
- Applying the ordinary monthly TER calculation to the final period.
- Leaving a bonus, allowance or taxable benefit out of the reconciliation because it was approved outside the regular payroll run.
- Assuming that the payroll register, earlier filings and Coretax's prefilled figures already agree.
- Using the later certificate handover date for the company's remittance or monthly filing.

## Frequently asked questions

### Is BPA1 the same document employees used to call 1721-A1?

BPA1 serves the role that 1721-A1 served for private-sector permanent employees. For current Coretax reporting, look for **BP A1** or **BPA1**. When checking an older certificate, use the period on the document to identify the rules and form that applied. [Former 1721-A1 procedure, PER-2/PJ/2024](https://www.pajak.go.id/id/peraturan/bentuk-dan-tata-cara-pembuatan-bukti-pemotongan-pajak-penghasilan-pasal-21-danatau-pajak) · [Current DJP rule, PER-12/PJ/2026](https://pajak.go.id/id/peraturan/perubahan-atas-peraturan-direktur-jenderal-pajak-nomor-11pj2025-tentang-ketentuan)

### Does a permanent employee need a BPA1 if their tax is zero?

The certificate is still required in specified cases where pay was made and the calculation results in zero withholding, including where income did not exceed the non-taxable income allowance. Verify the employee's circumstances and the return for their final period. [DJP reporting rules PER-11/PJ/2025, Article 8](https://www.pajak.go.id/id/peraturan/ketentuan-pelaporan-pajak-penghasilan-pajak-pertambahan-nilai-pajak-penjualan-atas-0) · [PMK 81/2024 as amended, Article 171](https://jdih.kemenkeu.go.id/dok/pmk-81-tahun-2024)

### What if a permanent employee changes employers during the year?

The former employer issues the BPA1 for the departure period. The new employer handles withholding for its own payroll and may be able to take the earlier certificate into account under the applicable rules. The employee should keep both certificates and check the figures in their annual return. [DJP guidance for employees who change jobs](https://pajak.go.id/coretaxpedia/bupot-pegawai-yang-pindah-tempat-kerja)

### Where can an employee find their BPA1 in Coretax?

In Coretax DJP, the employee can open *Bukti Potong Saya* under *Portal Saya* and *eBupot*, then filter by withholding type and period. If the certificate is missing or the figures differ from the employee's records, check which tax identifier and period the employer used. [DJP instructions for downloading withholding certificates](https://pajak.go.id/coretaxpedia/unduh-bukti-potong)

### Does a payroll refund mean the employee's annual return will show a refund?

The payroll refund settles excess withholding from that employer. The employee works out their overall tax position in their annual return using all relevant income and credits. A change of employer or other income can change the result. [PMK 168/2023, Article 21](https://jdih.kemenkeu.go.id/api/download/e60a82e0-b218-40f5-9d18-b924aa1e11ce/2023pmkeuangan168.pdf) · [DJP guidance on overwithholding and the individual return](https://pajak.go.id/id/artikel/di-formulir-1721-a1-lebih-potong-belum-tentu-spt-anda-lebih-bayar)
