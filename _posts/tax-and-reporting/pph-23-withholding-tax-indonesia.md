A supplier sends your Indonesian company an invoice marked “monthly services”. Before finance schedules the payment, someone needs to work out what the supplier did and whether the company should withhold part of the payment. *Pajak Penghasilan Pasal 23* (PPh 23) is Indonesia's Article 23 income tax withholding on certain domestic payments. This guide helps owners and finance teams, including those at foreign-investment companies (PT PMAs), make that decision and document it. [Indonesian Income Tax Law, Article 23](https://www.pajak.go.id/sites/default/files/2023-05/SDSN%202023%207.1_0.pdf#page=231)

## When PPh 23 applies to supplier payments

PPh 23 can apply when an Indonesian company makes a covered payment to an Indonesian resident taxpayer or an Indonesian permanent establishment (*Bentuk Usaha Tetap*, or BUT). The company is generally responsible for withholding when the payment falls within the rules. Covered categories include fees for management, technical and consulting services, other listed services, royalties, certain interest, and certain rentals of assets such as equipment. Each category has its own conditions and exclusions. [Indonesian Income Tax Law, Article 23](https://www.pajak.go.id/sites/default/files/2023-05/SDSN%202023%207.1_0.pdf#page=231) · [Ministry of Finance regulation on listed services, PMK 141/2015](https://www.pajak.go.id/index.php/id/peraturan/jenis-jasa-lain-sebagaimana-dimaksud-dalam-pasal-23-ayat-1-huruf-c-angka-2-undang-0)

Start by identifying the supplier's status and what each invoice line covers. An independent professional working as an individual may fall under [PPh 21](/blog/tax-and-reporting/pph-21-employee-income-tax-indonesia). Payments to non-residents may require a [PPh 26](/blog/tax-and-reporting/pph-26-withholding-tax-foreign-payments-indonesia) review. Qualifying land and building rent and construction services can fall under [PPh 4(2) final income tax](/blog/tax-and-reporting/pph-4-2-final-income-tax-indonesia). Review mixed contracts by checking each component and how it relates to the underlying agreement. [PMK 168/2023, Articles 3–6](https://jdih.kemenkeu.go.id/api/download/e60a82e0-b218-40f5-9d18-b924aa1e11ce/2023pmkeuangan168.pdf) · [Government Regulation PP 34/2017 on land and building rent](https://jdih.kemenkeu.go.id/dok/pp-34-tahun-2017/overview) · [Consolidated construction income tax rules PP 51/2008 as amended](https://jdih.kemenkeu.go.id/api/download/0de381f7-2f69-4bd2-91d2-ad1f88560e4a/PP%2051%202008.pdf)

## How to assess an invoice before paying it

1. **Confirm the recipient.** Collect the legal name, Indonesian taxpayer identification number (*Nomor Pokok Wajib Pajak*, or NPWP) where applicable, and any relevant tax-status or exemption documents. Match the supplier to the party named on the contract.
2. **Read the contract and itemised invoice together.** Identify what the supplier delivered, how the charges break down and when the amounts became payable. The Article 23 timing test can involve when the amount is paid, made available for payment or becomes due. Review the relevant dates even if accounts payable has yet to release a bank transfer. [Indonesian Income Tax Law, Article 23](https://www.pajak.go.id/sites/default/files/2023-05/SDSN%202023%207.1_0.pdf#page=231)
3. **Confirm the withholding basis.** Apply the rules for the transaction you identified. For other services covered by PMK 141/2015, the gross basis excludes VAT. Certain other exclusions depend on the service type and supporting documents. Where an invoice includes materials or third-party costs, check the evidence before excluding an amount. [PMK 141/2015, Article 1](https://www.pajak.go.id/index.php/id/peraturan/jenis-jasa-lain-sebagaimana-dimaksud-dalam-pasal-23-ayat-1-huruf-c-angka-2-undang-0)
4. **Record the withholding and give the supplier the evidence.** When withholding applies, reconcile the gross supplier amount, the amount paid to the supplier and the withholding payable to the tax authority. Prepare and provide the supplier with the applicable withholding certificate (*bukti potong*). [DJP reporting rules PER-11/PJ/2025, Articles 14–17](https://pajak.go.id/index.php/id/peraturan/ketentuan-pelaporan-pajak-penghasilan-pajak-pertambahan-nilai-pajak-penjualan-atas-0) · [DJP amendment PER-12/PJ/2026](https://pajak.go.id/id/peraturan/perubahan-atas-peraturan-direktur-jenderal-pajak-nomor-11pj2025-tentang-ketentuan)

## Illustrative example of a villa management invoice

A Bali villa management PT PMA receives a monthly “pool service” invoice from an Indonesian company. The attached agreement describes routine pool maintenance, and the invoice includes a separate line for chemicals. Pool maintenance is on the list of services that can attract PPh 23. The finance team checks the supplier's status and any applicable exemption documentation, then reviews whether the chemicals are part of the service charge or a material cost supported by purchase evidence under the rules. It keeps the contract and supplier documents with its calculation and withholding record. ## Paying and reporting PPh 23 each month

| Step | What the company does |
| --- | --- |
| Calculate and withhold | Confirm the category, basis and tax period for each relevant payment. Record the withholding and issue the applicable certificate. |
| Remit | Pay the tax due by the 15th of the following month under the ordinary deadline. Save the validated payment evidence. |
| File | Report PPh 23 in the unified monthly income tax return (*SPT Masa PPh Unifikasi*) through Coretax DJP as applicable, generally within 20 days after the tax period ends. Save the filing acknowledgement. |
| Reconcile | Match the return and supporting certificates to the supplier ledger, tax account and payment evidence. |

When a deadline falls on a holiday covered by the rules, payment or filing can generally take place by the next working day. The unified return can cover more than PPh 23. Check whether any tax covered by that return requires a withholding or collection record or self-payment for the period, even when there were zero PPh 23 deductions. [PMK 81/2024 as amended, Articles 94, 100, 171 and 173](https://jdih.kemenkeu.go.id/dok/pmk-81-tahun-2024) · [DJP guidance on unified withholding and reporting](https://www.pajak.go.id/index.php/id/berita/bahas-e-bupot-unifikasi-kpp-tangerang-barat-hadirkan-siniar-pajak) · [DJP reporting amendment PER-12/PJ/2026](https://pajak.go.id/id/peraturan/perubahan-atas-peraturan-direktur-jenderal-pajak-nomor-11pj2025-tentang-ketentuan)

## Common PPh 23 mistakes

- Applying the same treatment to every invoice described as “services”.
- Using the supplier's trading name alone to decide the recipient's tax status.
- Treating every charge described as “rent” as equipment rental without reading the agreement.
- Deducting a separately listed expense from the withholding basis without checking the supporting documents.
- Recording the net bank transfer as the supplier cost and losing track of the gross invoice, withholding and certificate.

## Frequently asked questions

### Do management fees attract PPh 23?

Management services are a category expressly listed in Article 23. When an Indonesian business owes a management fee to a domestic provider, it should confirm the recipient's status, what the agreement covers and whether an exclusion applies. A mixed invoice can include other charges that need their own review. [Indonesian Income Tax Law, Article 23](https://www.pajak.go.id/sites/default/files/2023-05/SDSN%202023%207.1_0.pdf#page=231)

### Who is responsible for PPh 23 withholding?

For a covered payment made by an Indonesian company, the payer generally withholds the tax, remits it and handles the relevant monthly reporting. It also provides the supplier with the withholding certificate, which the supplier can use as evidence when claiming an eligible tax credit in its annual return. [Indonesian Income Tax Law, Article 23](https://www.pajak.go.id/sites/default/files/2023-05/SDSN%202023%207.1_0.pdf#page=231) · [DJP reporting rules PER-11/PJ/2025, Article 15](https://pajak.go.id/index.php/id/peraturan/ketentuan-pelaporan-pajak-penghasilan-pajak-pertambahan-nilai-pajak-penjualan-atas-0) · [DJP guidance on tax credits](https://pajak.go.id/id/kredit-pajak)

### How is PPh 23 different from PPh 4(2)?

PPh 23 withheld on covered income can generally count as a credit against the recipient's annual income tax, subject to the credit rules. PPh 4(2) is final income tax for specified income, including qualifying land and building rent and construction services. Identify what the agreement actually covers before choosing a withholding category. [DJP guidance on tax credits](https://pajak.go.id/id/kredit-pajak) · [Government Regulation PP 34/2017](https://jdih.kemenkeu.go.id/dok/pp-34-tahun-2017/overview) · [Consolidated construction income tax rules PP 51/2008 as amended](https://jdih.kemenkeu.go.id/api/download/0de381f7-2f69-4bd2-91d2-ad1f88560e4a/PP%2051%202008.pdf)

### Does zero PPh 23 mean there is no monthly return to file?

Check all taxes covered by the unified return, including situations where a certificate is required with zero tax withheld. If none of those taxes requires a withholding or collection certificate or self-payment for the period, the unified monthly filing requirement generally does not apply. [PMK 81/2024 as amended, Article 171(8)](https://jdih.kemenkeu.go.id/dok/pmk-81-tahun-2024) · [DJP reporting rules PER-11/PJ/2025, Article 21](https://pajak.go.id/index.php/id/peraturan/ketentuan-pelaporan-pajak-penghasilan-pajak-pertambahan-nilai-pajak-penjualan-atas-0)
