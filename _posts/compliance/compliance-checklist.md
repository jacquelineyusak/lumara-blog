Company compliance is a collection of recurring responsibilities: reports, renewals, registrations, documents, and changes that need to be reflected in the right places.

A useful checklist keeps four questions visible:

- What is required?
- When does it need attention?
- Which document or piece of information supports it?
- Who owns the next step?

## Review changes, not just deadlines

The biggest gaps often appear after a change in the business. A new employee, a new activity, a new location, or a change in ownership can affect which records and registrations need to be reviewed.

Add a short compliance check to your business-change process. It is a small habit that helps the checklist stay current as the company evolves.
