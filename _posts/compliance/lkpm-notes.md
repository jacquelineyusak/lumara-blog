LKPM preparation starts with a consistent record of what changed in the business. Before a reporting period closes, gather the company information, project activity and supporting records that the person preparing the report will need. The checklist below is a working handover pack; the applicable form and reporting obligation must be confirmed for the business.

## Confirm the reporting context first

Record the company’s NIB, the activity or project being reported, its location, and the reporting period. Ask the responsible person to confirm the business classification and project stage before gathering figures for the wrong form. The [official OSS guide directory](https://oss.go.id/id/panduan) provides separate LKPM guidance for different business categories and project stages.

Keep a copy of the previous submission and any follow-up comments beside the current working file. That makes changes easier to identify and helps avoid treating a cumulative figure as a new amount for the current period.

## Build one preparation folder

Use a folder for each period with these sections:

| Section | What to gather | What to check before handover |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Company and project | Current company identifiers, activity and project-location details | Do the working records match the information held in OSS? |
| Investment activity | A schedule of relevant expenditure and its supporting documents | Can each entry be traced to a document and the correct period? |
| Staffing | The working headcount record and changes during the period | Has the person responsible for staffing checked the information? |
| Business activity | A summary of operational changes, project progress and relevant business records | Are explanations clear enough for someone outside daily operations? |
| Open issues | Missing documents, questions and previous reviewer comments | Does each issue have an owner and a next action? |
| Submission record | The final approved figures and evidence of submission or follow-up | Can the next preparer see what was submitted and what remains open? |

This folder structure is a suggested internal process. It is not a claim that every business must attach the same documents or complete the same fields.

## Reconcile before handing over

Compare the working schedule with the supporting records. Flag unexplained differences, duplicate entries and transactions that belong to another period. Keep uncertain items visible in an exceptions list rather than silently changing a figure to make totals match.

For example, if a purchase is recorded in two files, identify which entry is the source record and reference it once in the working schedule. If a figure includes earlier periods, label it clearly so the preparer can determine how it belongs in the report.

## Agree who approves and follows up

Assign one person to provide missing information, one to review the completed working figures and one to monitor the submission status. Save the final version separately from drafts. After submission, check whether further action or correction is requested; a sent report and a completed follow-up process are different stages of work.

The [official OSS LKPM preparation-stage guide](https://s3.oss.go.id/oss/cms/Laporan-Kegiatan-Penanaman-Modal---Non-Usaha-Mikro-dan-Kecil-%28LKPM---Non-UMK%29-Tahap-Konstruksi_Persiapan-a583d1e62d974a9f93e1d74d6922cab5.pdf) describes the reporting workflow for that specific stage. Use the guide that matches your business rather than assuming this one applies to every company.

## Check the current reporting window

Do not reuse an old reporting date without checking it. Confirm the current period and window in OSS and the relevant official announcement. This preparation guide does not set a statutory deadline or determine whether a particular company must report.
